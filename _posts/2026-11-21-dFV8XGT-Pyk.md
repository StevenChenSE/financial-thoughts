## Central Banks and the Return of Gold as Collateral

A fundamental shift in global finance is underway: central banks are no longer buying gold merely for liquidity but are integrating it back into the international collateral framework.

Data from the World Gold Council and Bloomberg indicates a massive structural change in central bank buying patterns starting in 2022, following Russia’s special military operation in Ukraine and the subsequent freezing of Russian assets by Western alliances.
*   **Pre-2022:** Central banks often bought gold to sell later for USD liquidity.
*   **Post-2022:** Buying has remained robust even as prices doubled from $1,600–$1,800 to over $3,500+.

This suggests central banks view physical gold as a viable international collateral asset alongside US Treasury bonds. Recent developments support this thesis:
