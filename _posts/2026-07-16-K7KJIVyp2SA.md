## Colin Catal Discusses Warrant Leverage, Junior Mining Capital, and Portfolio Performance at the Rick Rule Symposium

At the Rick Rule Symposium in Boca Raton, Florida, Colin Catal, CEO of Palisades Gold Corp (TSXV: PALI), presented a detailed overview of his company's distinctive investment approach. Operating as an active investment firm rather than a traditional producer, Palisades Gold focuses on high-volume financing and a proprietary warrant strategy designed to capture outsized leverage in the junior mining sector. Catal highlighted the firm's rapid deployment of capital, its unique position relative to ETFs, and the performance of a massive warrant book that underpins the company's value proposition.

### The Palisades Gold Business Model

Palisades Gold distinguishes itself through a dual focus on active financing and company building. Over the past 18 months, the firm has participated in approximately 450 financings, deploying roughly $500 million into the sector. The investment thesis centers on identifying high-quality explorers, developers, and producers while securing warrant coverage in exchange for capital. Catal emphasizes that this structure provides Palisades' shareholders with access to the leverage and upside potential inherent in warrants, which are often unavailable to public equity holders.

Beyond financing, Palisades is an active builder of companies. The firm founded Newfound Gold Corp, where Catal served as CEO for four and a half years, and subsequently spun out entities including Earth Labs, Nevada King, Made in America Gold, and Radio Fuels. This hands-on approach allows the company to cultivate assets from the ground up while maintaining a diversified investment portfolio.

### Capital Availability and Shifting Market Dynamics

Addressing the state of capital in the junior mining space, Catal firmly rejects the notion that funding is unavailable. However, he notes significant shifts in market dynamics since the fourth quarter of 2025 into the first quarter of 2026. While capital remains accessible for high-quality issuers, the terms have evolved. Securing warrants with long durations has become more challenging, and unit prices have risen as share prices have improved. Catal observes increased competition among investors, a stark contrast to the environment of the previous year, indicating a thaw in sentiment and a return of capital writers to the sector.

### The Warrant Strategy and Portfolio Scale

Central to Palisades Gold's strategy is its warrant portfolio, which Catal describes as the largest book of its kind held by a public company. The firm holds 1.7 billion warrants across 330 companies, averaging 22 warrants per share of PALI. This scale offers individual investors a diversified exposure that would be impractical to replicate independently.

The mechanics of the strategy involve subscribing to financing units, selling the equity portion to recoup the principal investment, and retaining the warrants. This approach limits downside risk while preserving the optionality to benefit from future appreciation. Catal cited Galantis Gold as a prime example: Palisades sold its shares at a substantial profit, retaining warrants exercisable at 12 cents against a stock trading at 50 cents. This left the firm with significant unrealized value and no capital at risk, positioning the warrants to capture further upside if the stock continues to rise.

### Performance Metrics

The warrant strategy has generated notable returns. As of the interview, 32% of the 450 warrants held are in the money. The intrinsic value of the warrant portfolio surged from approximately $2.5 million in August 2025 to $130 million by January 2026, representing a 25x return on the warrant component alone. Although the value has since pulled back to $60 million, it remains significantly higher than the baseline. Catal attributes this performance to the initial accumulation phase during a period of low investor interest and the subsequent rise in the gold market.

### Advantages Over ETFs

Catal argues that Palisades Gold fills a critical niche that ETFs cannot serve. Exchange-traded funds are generally restricted from participating in private financings, meaning they cannot acquire the leverage provided by warrants. Additionally, ETFs often avoid companies with market caps below $50 million, despite these micro-caps frequently delivering the highest returns. As an investment company, Palisades can engage directly in financings, build a basket of sub-$50 million assets, and utilize warrants to amplify returns, offering a product distinct from broad-based index funds like the PDXJ.

### Diversified Commodity Exposure

Despite the name Palisades Gold, the portfolio is commodity-agnostic, mirroring the composition of the Toronto Venture Exchange. Holdings span gold, copper, silver, uranium, and battery metals. Key positions include First Majestic Silver, Copper Giant, Global Atomic, Talisker Metals, and Surge Battery Metals. Catal emphasizes that the firm focuses on the terms of the investment rather than betting on specific commodities, believing that a bull market will lift various sectors in a rotating manner. The portfolio is viewed as a long-term holding designed to benefit from broad capital flows into the resource sector.

### Leadership and Board of Directors

Palisades Gold is led by CEO Colin Catal, who founded the company and previously built Newfound Gold into a billion-dollar market cap enterprise. The management team includes Phil O'Neal, a veteran of the uranium space with ties to Rick Rule and Doug Casey, and CFO Jeff Steber. The board of directors brings diverse expertise, featuring Gregor Gregerson, founder of Silver Bullion SG and operator of the world's largest silver vault; Betty Harrison, a retired head lawyer at Farris LLP; and Colonel Douglas McGregor, a decorated combat veteran and former adviser to the Secretary of Defense who advocates for domestic metal supply chains.

### Warrant Maturity and Future Outlook

The weighted average duration of the warrant portfolio is 27 months, with expirations extending into November 2028. Catal addresses the concern that junior miners may require years to hit milestones, noting that the firm plans to exercise warrants for companies with strong prospects as they approach expiry, converting them into long-term equity positions. This strategy allows Palisades to stack the odds in its favor while maintaining flexibility.

Looking ahead, Catal remains bullish on the gold market but notes that retail investor interest has yet to materialize. He compares current conditions to the TSX Venture Exchange's performance in the 2000s, suggesting the sector is still starved for capital. Palisades Gold aims to capitalize on this opportunity, waiting for the next wave of capital flows to drive further appreciation in its warrant book. For investors new to the resource space, Catal recommends starting with the educational content provided by Rick Rule, whose insights continue to influence the firm's strategy.

Palisades Gold Corp trades under the ticker PALI on the TSX Venture Exchange.
