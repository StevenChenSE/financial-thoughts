Michell maps the strategies of what he terms the "criminal oligarch cabal" onto the seven stages of alchemy. The process begins with **Calcination**, where the elite, influenced by the 1971 Limits to Growth report, view the population as "useless eaters" consuming resources. This is followed by **Dissolution**, a long-range social engineering effort that demoralizes societal institutions and creates untenable circumstances for the public.

The stage of **Separation** involves the destruction of the family unit and community trust, exacerbated by issues like gender dysmorphia and historical guilt. During **Conjunction**, proposals like the Great Reset, 15-minute cities, and Universal Basic Income are presented as solutions. **Fermentation** requires the public to believe in and defend these systems, much like historical regimes did. **Distillation** refines the plan as the public comes on board, leading to **Coagulation**, where the new system takes hold in the collective consciousness, turning people into "true believers" who no longer need to be coerced.

## Energy, Resources, and the Failure of the Current System

A critical component of Michell's analysis is the energy transition. He highlights his peer-reviewed research indicating that phasing out fossil fuels creates a massive energy and mineral shortfall. Current renewable technologies, including wind, solar, and hydrogen, fail to meet the energy return on energy invested (EROEI) required to sustain modern society. In contrast, Michell points to Thorium Molten Salt Reactors (Generation 4 nuclear) as a viable solution, boasting an EROEI of 171 to 1, far superior to fossil fuels (27:1) and renewables (~2:1).

He argues that the current path is unsustainable due to material constraints. The demand for critical raw materials like rare earth elements and semiconductors for AI data centers, electric vehicles, and military applications exceeds available supply. This scarcity, combined with a hyperinflationary spiral, renders traditional market mechanisms meaningless, setting the stage for conflict over resources.

## The Four Archetypes of Society

Michell predicts that as the global system fractures, society will split into four distinct groups, akin to factions in the RPG *Shadowrun*, which accurately predicted many modern technological trends.

1.  **Cornucopians:** Those who defend the existing system to the bitter end, ignoring evident collapse ("the dog in the room on fire").
2.  **Vikings (Pirates):** Those who, seeing the system fail, resort to taking resources from others, relying on force rather than production.
3.  **Preppers:** Individuals who focus on self-sufficiency, securing local food, energy, and basic needs, akin to the Amish approach to technology.
4.  **Arcadians:** Those who seek to maintain the best aspects of modern society—education, human rights, and technology—while transitioning to a sustainable, localized model. Michell identifies with this group, advocating for a long-range plan to preserve human capability and empathy.

## The Mass Awakening and Ascension

In response to the elite's alchemical plan, Michell proposes a counter-process of "Ascension." Through **Separation**, the public understands how the world truly works and removes corrupted systems. In **Conjunction**, communities globally collaborate to create a new plan, ensuring it has broad trust and support. **Fermentation** involves breathing life into this new system through local, national, and international review. **Distillation** refines the plan, potentially drawing inspiration from documents like the US Constitution, leading to **Coagulation**, where the new system becomes self-sufficient and resilient against bad actors.

Michell emphasizes that the window for change is narrow. The elite are rolling out digital IDs, Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs), and AI surveillance to enforce compliance. However, he remains skeptical of their success, noting that the average person is more educated and cynical than in the past. The failure of the Soviet Union, linked to its peak in oil production, serves as a cautionary tale: energy stability is linked to societal stability.

The future may involve parallel systems competing for survival, with wealth redefined not by fiat currency but by access to fertile land, industrial capacity, and community skill sets. Michell urges viewers to take control of their destiny, maintain empathy, and prepare for a transformation that may be difficult but is necessary for the preservation of human sovereignty and dignity.
