## Smart Money Targets High Relative Strength Plays in Gold Sector

Reporting from the Rick Rule Symposium in Boca Raton, Daniel of *Hot Trades of the Day* outlines a strategic approach to the gold mining sector. With gold testing critical technical levels, the focus shifts to three miners across large, mid, and small caps that are demonstrating exceptional relative strength. These stocks are positioned to attract smart money flows as the market searches for leaders capable of decoupling from broader metal weakness.

### Gold Market Context and Strategy

Gold price action remains a key driver for miner performance. The metal recently tested the $4,215 resistance area, resulting in a "doink" off the established downtrend. Market participants are closely watching whether this rejection signals the exhaustion of selling pressure or the onset of a deeper correction.

Technical analysis highlights a potential head and shoulders formation if gold holds support around $4,720, though $4,000 remains the definitive floor. The strategy involves initiating starter positions in high-conviction miners, anticipating that the bottoming process is underway without a severe undercut. However, risk management remains paramount; positions can be scaled up if an undercut occurs, providing better entry points.

The core thesis relies on the "strength to strength" theme. After prolonged corrections, capital tends to flow into stocks that maintain relative strength against the underlying commodity. These leaders often trigger fresh breakout signals and draw retail interest, creating momentum opportunities for swing trades or longer-term investments.

### SSR Mining: Large Cap Resilience

**SSR Mining (SSRM)** stands out as a large-cap option with a market capitalization of approximately $6 billion USD. Despite gold's decline in 2026, SSRM's chart exhibits remarkable resilience. The stock is trading above its 200-day moving average and has broken out of the long-term downtrend that originated during the severe winter sell-off of mining stocks in 2024.

SSR Mining's performance suggests institutional accumulation. The stock peaked later than the gold price, reacting to the broader sell-off before establishing new highs. Key technical levels to monitor include a gap fill near 2880 and a secondary gap around 2720. A retest of the 200-day moving average could also present a risk-defined entry, though the stock currently sits well above this support, indicating a bullish structure.

### Banyan Gold: Mid-Cap Breakout Candidate

**Banyan Gold (BGL)**, a mid-cap company valued at roughly $700 million CAD and present at the Rick Rule Symposium, is displaying an aggressive chart. The stock is trading near all-time highs, significantly outperforming the metal. BGL holds positions above both the 50-day and 200-day moving averages, confirming a robust uptrend.

A critical technical development is the breakout above a yellow resistance zone on the close. A sustained price action above 165, coupled with gold avoiding new lows, signals that Banyan Gold could be poised for a significant run. The relative strength here is so pronounced that the chart appears bullish regardless of gold's immediate direction, suggesting strong independent momentum.

### Cabral Gold: Small Cap Development Play

**Cabral Gold (CAB)** represents the small-cap segment and is a developer close to production with a project in Mexico. This stock has been in a 25% uptrend since December 2024 and is currently held by the analyst. Cabral Gold is trading above its 50-day and 200-day moving averages, with a washout pattern that remains intact.

Cabral Gold is attempting to break through a resistance zone while demonstrating strength against a weaker gold market. The stock's ability to rise while gold faces pressure highlights its internal strength and building momentum. If gold stabilizes and finds a bottom, Cabral Gold is well-positioned to lead the small-cap recovery, offering high upside potential for swing traders and investors.

### Conclusion: Positioning for the Turn

The analysis of SSR Mining, Banyan Gold, and Cabral Gold underscores the importance of relative strength in selecting mining equities. These stocks are not merely following gold; they are leading the sector with distinct technical breakouts and resilience.

If gold stabilizes within the $4,200 to $4,000 range, these miners offer compelling opportunities. Smart money is likely flowing into these strong performers, creating a favorable environment for swing trades and potential long-term investments. Traders should monitor gold's behavior in the coming days to confirm the bottoming process, but the strength displayed by these three miners suggests they are ready to run as market sentiment improves.
