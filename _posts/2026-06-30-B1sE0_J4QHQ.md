## Expert Analysis on Precious Metals, Mining Stocks, and Market Outlook for Mid-2026

The precious metals market is experiencing significant turbulence as gold hovers near the critical $4,000 level. In a recent interview on Capital, Florian Grun from Midas Touch Consulting provided comprehensive analysis on the state of gold, silver, mining stocks, and broader market conditions. With gold having dropped to around $3,958 and showing signs of capitulation, investors are wondering whether the bottom is near or if more pain lies ahead.

The timing of this analysis—June 29, 2026—places us at a critical juncture where seasonal factors, technical indicators, and broader market dynamics are converging to create a complex environment for precious metals investors. Understanding these interconnected elements is essential for making informed decisions in the coming weeks and months.

---

## Gold Market: Finding Bottom or Falling Further?

Gold has been on a downward trajectory, currently testing the psychologically important $4,000 level. The metal briefly dropped below this threshold last week, reaching approximately $3,958-3,959 before recovering. According to Grun, we are likely approaching the typical early summer bottom, though the final lows may not yet be in place.

"We've seen some capitulation over the last few weeks, especially once gold lost the $4,400 support a few weeks ago and then got this first flush lower—a nice sharp recovery—and then FOMC meeting and then gold came back down under pressure," Grun explained. The key question is whether the recent lows represent the final bottom or if prices will test these levels again before establishing sustainable support.

Technical analysis suggests the $3,900-4,000 zone should provide meaningful support based on price action from autumn of the previous year. The weekly and daily charts show the market is oversold, and most leveraged traders have likely already sold their positions. This suggests a potential bounce could materialize in the coming one to three weeks, possibly extending into August or even September.

However, Grun cautioned that if the $3,900 support level breaks decisively—with backing from the November autumn price action—the next significant support line sits around $3,400. "That's a pretty steep drop if it breaks below this key support level," he noted.

The broader correction that began at the end of January may not be complete. Grun's current thinking involves a bottom-building process in the near term, followed by a summer rally, and then potentially another leg lower before the final bottom establishes itself later in the year—potentially in October or even mid-December, which historically marks important seasonal lows.

## Silver Analysis and the Gold/Silver Ratio

The silver-to-gold ratio currently trades around 69-70, approximately 1.4% of gold's price. This ratio serves as an important indicator of the health of the precious metals bull market. Grun prefers the gold-to-silver ratio, which currently sits around 69-70, having recovered from lows of 43 reached in early 2026 and January.

Looking at the ratio's trajectory, Grun identified strong support around 78-79, with potential to reach even 80 in the later summer months. "It just clearly confirms that the metals are in a correction," he stated. "Silver is underperforming on the upside and outperforming on the downside. That is unfortunately the setup we are in right now."

For silver itself, the $50 level represents a critical round number and important breakout zone that dates back to the 1980 and 2011 highs. When zooming out, this level connects to a significant cup and handle pattern formation. The first target above current levels sits around $100, while the ultimate target from this large cup pattern could reach $250-$500.

"I really have to give a disclaimer here in terms of timing—I think this will take a while," Grun cautioned. "This can easily take two, three, five years, maybe even longer." The current correction that began in late January could still continue for some time, meaning investors should temper expectations for immediate explosive gains.

## Mining Stocks: Mixed Signals from the Sector

The ARS (a major mining stocks index) has outperformed gold since mid-2024 but has been underperforming on the monthly candlesticks since approximately February. From a closer perspective, the sector has been choppy and sideways since December, offering limited actionable signals.

Top-tier mining stocks like Agnico Eagle have held up relatively better over the past two weeks, which could indicate the sector is already sniffing out an approaching recovery. However, the vast majority of mining stocks have experienced significant selloffs. With the market peak arriving in early January, many investors who bought in recent months are likely underwater, as are those who purchased during the last few months of the previous year.

The timing consideration is particularly important for mining stocks. End of June and early July traditionally represents a period where aggressive positioning in the sector may not be rewarded. Looking ahead to October and especially November and December, tax loss selling could force underwater positions to be liquidated, creating additional pressure.

Grun's recommendation is measured: "I still don't think it's time to like aggressively load up on the mining stocks. But at the same time, here and there there are some interesting opportunities already. Again, with a long-term mindset and approach, you can nibble at these levels."

## Broader Market Warning Signs

Beyond precious metals, Grun expressed significant concerns about the general stock market. The S&P 500 experienced a substantial rally following the Iran crisis but now appears to be in a topping pattern. The semiconductor sector has gone parabolic since April of the previous year, more than doubling within a year—a development Grun views as a major warning signal.

The SpaceX IPO provides an instructive example. After shooting up from 150 to 226, the stock has essentially given back all its gains. "It's not a very sexy chart, especially if it's an IPO," Grun noted. Early investors and insiders were able to exit to retail buyers, a pattern he sees as concerning.

The AI sector presents additional concerns. Grun sees numerous indicators suggesting the market may be approaching a period where this significant bubble could begin deflating. While he emphasizes he doesn't want to call an exact top or encourage short positions—acknowledging the setup could continue volatile sideways movement for weeks—the risk-reward for the stock market no longer favors aggressive positioning.

The hyperscaler capex competition between Western tech giants and Chinese counterparts adds another layer of complexity. When stock markets come under pressure, precious metals rarely continue their own upward trajectory independently. "Usually everything's connected, and if liquidity is disappearing, then people investors also sell their gold positions, especially the ETF positions," Grun explained.

## Oil Market: Oversold and Filling the Gap

Oil has experienced a dramatic reversal, dropping from highs around $120 to current levels near $70. This decline has largely erased gains seen prior to the Iran war, with pre-conflict prices around $67—meaning current levels are not far from that baseline.

The market has essentially filled the gap left open when the Iran conflict began in late February. Grun expressed surprise at the depth and speed of the decline but noted that oil is now extremely oversold and testing former breakout levels as support.

"I don't think the situation in the Middle East is resolved at all," Grun stated, suggesting underlying fundamental factors may support a recovery. While he expressed uncertainty about whether oil can return directly to $120, the current oversold condition and unfinished geopolitical tensions could set the stage for a rebound.

## Strategic Recommendations for the Summer Months

Given the current environment, Grun offered several strategic considerations for investors:

**For Physical Gold and Silver Buyers:** Around $4,000 for gold represents an interesting entry point for building physical positions and increasing stacks. This level offers reasonable value for long-term investors willing to accumulate gradually.

**For Trading-Oriented Investors:** Caution remains warranted. July typically brings low trading volumes as people take holidays, particularly in Europe where summer heat encourages beach outings. "It's not the time where you need to usually be very aggressive in the markets," Grun advised.

**For Mining Stock Investors:** A patient approach makes sense. While selective opportunities exist for long-term buyers, there's no urgency to be heavily or fully invested. The broader market weakness and potential for tax loss selling later in the year suggest waiting for better entry points.

**General Market Positioning:** September and October traditionally represent the worst months for stock markets, and this seasonal weakness typically builds through the summer. A weaker summer could set up particularly challenging conditions into autumn. "Take it easy, step back. No need to be fully invested in these markets," Grun concluded.

## Summary

The precious metals market is at a critical inflection point. Gold appears oversold near key support around $4,000, with potential for a bottom-building process to conclude in the coming one to three weeks, setting up a possible summer rally into August or September. However, the broader correction may not be complete, with another leg lower possible before final lows establish themselves later in the year.

Silver continues to underperform, with the gold-to-silver ratio confirming a correction phase. The white metal may eventually target the $50 level and beyond as part of a long-term bullish structure, but patience is required given the current weakness.

Mining stocks offer selective opportunities but warrant cautious positioning, particularly given potential tax loss selling in the autumn months. Meanwhile, the broader stock market exhibits concerning topping patterns, with parabolic moves in semiconductors and AI potentially signaling bubble conditions that could affect all markets when they reverse.

For investors, the message is clear: summer months call for measured positioning, not aggressive action. Physical metal accumulation at current prices makes sense for long-term investors, while traders should maintain flexibility and avoid overcommitment. The coming weeks will provide crucial clues about whether the anticipated summer bounce materializes or if additional downside remains before the correction concludes.
