## Understanding Investments

Investing is one of the most powerful ways to build wealth over time. Here's what beginners need to know.

## Types of Investments

### Stocks

When you buy a stock, you own a small piece of that company. Stocks have historically provided higher returns than other investments over the long term.

### Bonds

Bonds are loans you make to governments or companies. They provide regular income through interest payments.

### Index Funds

Index funds track a market index like the S&P 500. They offer diversification and low fees.

## Getting Started

1. **Open a brokerage account** - Compare fees and features
2. **Start with index funds** - Great for beginners
3. **Dollar-cost average** - Invest regularly regardless of market conditions
4. **Diversify** - Don't put all your eggs in one basket

## Risk Management

Always consider your risk tolerance and investment timeline. Younger investors can take more risks since they have time to recover from market downturns.
