## Market Status and Key Levels
The crude oil market has recently provided the anticipated additional high, signaling the potential completion of a corrective rally. This update focuses on the technical structure of both WTI and Brent crude, highlighting critical resistance and support levels that will determine the next major market move.

## WTI Crude Technical Analysis
On the WTI chart, the price action has confirmed a full **ABC structure** moving upward from the low established on July 2nd. This structure consists of:
*   **Wave A:** An upward move in three sub-waves.
*   **Wave B:** A downward correction.
*   **Wave C:** The recent upward move completing the pattern.

The current focus is on the resistance zones between **$80.60** and **$84.20**. While a slight push higher into Monday is possible, traders should closely monitor the **Fibonacci resistance levels at $82.93 and $84.27**.

### Critical Support and Bearish Scenario
The local uptrend remains intact as long as the price holds above the last swing low at **$77.20**. However, a break below this level would serve as the first indication that a top has formed. In a wider Elliott Wave scenario, this could mark the end of an internal fourth wave, potentially leading to a fifth wave down toward the support area between **$68 and $59.70**. Until that break occurs, the bullish bias remains, but caution is advised near resistance.

## Brent Crude Technical Analysis
The Brent crude chart mirrors the WTI structure, showing a very similar wave count. Analyzing the 4-hour timeframe, the market has also achieved the "one more high" target within an ABC recovery rally.

### Key Levels for Brent
*   **Resistance:** **$88.82** is the key resistance level. Whether the price breaks briefly above this level is considered secondary to the wave count confirmation.
*   **Support:** The critical support level is at **$84**.

Similar to WTI, there is no sign of a top until the market breaks below the last swing low at $84. The local uptrend is still in play, and a break below this red line would be required to confirm a reversal.

## Outlook for Next Week
The coming week is expected to be decisive for the crude oil market. Traders should watch for signs of a pullback after this recent high. The market is likely to choose between two scenarios:
1.  **White Scenario (Bullish):** Continuation of the upward momentum.
2.  **Orange Count (Bearish):** Formation of one more low, indicating a larger correction is resuming.

## Summary
Both WTI and Brent crude have completed an ABC corrective rally, reaching their targeted highs. The immediate focus shifts to key resistance levels at $82.93/$84.27 for WTI and $88.82 for Brent. A decisive break below $77.20 (WTI) and $84 (Brent) is required to confirm a top and potential deeper correction. Until then, the local uptrend remains valid, but traders should prepare for increased volatility and potential trend reversal signals next week.
