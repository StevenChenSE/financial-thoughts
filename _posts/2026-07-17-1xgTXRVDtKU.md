## Strategic Asset Allocation Amid Geopolitical Shifts and Valuation Extremes

**Introduction**
In a recent market outlook, Chance Finucane, CIO of Oxbow Advisors, detailed a strategic pivot away from overextended momentum trades toward fundamentally grounded sectors. With the artificial intelligence trade showing signs of fatigue and geopolitical tensions reshaping commodity markets, Oxbow is recalibrating portfolios to prioritize risk-adjusted returns. Finucane’s analysis highlights a disciplined approach to navigating valuation extremes, inflation dynamics, and shifting interest rate expectations, while outlining a cautious stance heading into 2027.

### The Unwind of the AI Trade and Semiconductor Risks
The past year has been dominated by the AI trade, which has accounted for nearly half of the S&P 500’s market capitalization and driven the majority of returns. However, Oxbow Advisors views this concentration as heavily overvalued. Finucane noted that high-quality semiconductor companies, which comprise roughly 12 to 15 major players, carry a projected 40% downside risk in a typical 20% to 25% index bear market. Oxbow’s investment mandate requires a maximum projected downside of 20% for new equity positions, making the current AI and semiconductor landscape unattractive.

The firm also highlighted the disconnect between private marketplace valuations and historical public offerings. While companies like SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic capture retail imagination, their implied valuations of 20 to 70 times revenue starkly contrast with historical norms like Google’s IPO at 8.5 times revenue. Oxbow avoids riding momentum waves, preferring to wait for cyclical normalization. The recent pullback in semiconductor stocks despite strong earnings reports signals that investors are already pricing in a multi-year earnings plateau, prompting Oxbow to stay sidelined until risk-reward profiles improve.

### Strategic Rotation into Energy and Precious Metals
As momentum capital rotates out of tech, Oxbow has been incrementally adding to unloved sectors, specifically energy and precious metals. After trimming precious metals exposure in January when gold spiked toward $5,500 per ounce and silver approached $116, the firm identified buying opportunities as prices retreated. Finucane outlined clear entry thresholds: gold near $4,000 and silver around $60. Oxbow’s target allocation for precious metals sits at approximately 10% of the high-income strategy, evenly split between physical metals and mining/royalty companies.

The energy thesis remains equally compelling. Following an unexpected surge above $110 per barrel triggered by the Iran war, Oxbow prudently trimmed extended positions. As oil stabilized near $80, the firm began rebuilding exposure. Finucane emphasized that North American oil and gas producers, refiners, and pipeline operators remain relatively insulated from Middle Eastern supply disruptions due to domestic and Canadian production. Furthermore, modern management teams have optimized capital allocation, prioritizing shareholder returns through dividends and buybacks. Oxbow has also expanded into oil service businesses, anticipating increased drilling and infrastructure repair demand globally.

### Geopolitical Headwinds and Inflation Dynamics
The resumption of the Iran war and ongoing conflict in Ukraine have accelerated a broader structural shift that began in 2020. Finucane described the current decade as an era of heightened geopolitical volatility, stickier inflation, and more erratic interest rate movements. Tariffs and trade protectionism have temporarily taken a backseat to immediate security concerns, but the underlying macro backdrop remains challenging.

Consumer weakness persists as inflation continues to outpace wage growth, echoing the headwinds seen in 2022. Oxbow advises caution in consumer discretionary investments, favoring businesses with structural tailwinds over those reliant on optional spending. The firm’s approach to geopolitical shocks is reactive but disciplined: trim beneficiaries of rapid, speculation-driven spikes, monitor downside exposure across the portfolio, and reinvest once valuations normalize. This nimble yet principles-driven framework allows Oxbow to navigate sudden market dislocations without abandoning long-term positioning.

### Fixed Income Strategy and the Bond Curve
Oxbow’s fixed income allocation reflects a clear distinction between short-term and long-term Treasuries. Since 2020, the firm has identified a structural regime where 10-year and 30-year Treasury yields are poised to trend higher, creating headwinds for long-duration bond prices. Consequently, Oxbow has shifted its focus to maturities of three years or less, including short-term Treasuries, investment-grade corporate bonds, and municipal bonds when client-specific needs arise.

This duration discipline allows the firm to quickly roll maturing assets into higher-yielding instruments as inflation and rates adjust. Recently, as the two-year Treasury yield climbed above 4%, Oxbow locked in a significant portion of client assets at that level. This positions portfolios to benefit from potential Federal Reserve rate cuts if inflation moderates, while protecting against prolonged stickiness. Monitoring tools like the CME Fed Watch Tool indicate market uncertainty around potential rate hikes, but Oxbow maintains that the Fed will likely wait for sustained inflation traction before easing policy.

### Market Mechanics and the 2027 Outlook
Approximately 60% of current market trading activity is driven by momentum players, including quantitative funds and retail investors chasing the next trending sector. As these traders rotate out of semiconductors, capital has flowed into financials, healthcare, utilities, and consumer staples. Oxbow leverages this behavior by accumulating high-quality names in these lagging sectors, positioning itself ahead of potential momentum flips.

Looking ahead, Finucane warned of a potential deeper correction by 2027. The firm anticipates that cyclical deceleration in growth and inflation normalization against the elevated first-half benchmarks will create a challenging environment for risky assets. While short-term volatility may persist, Oxbow’s strategy remains anchored in patience, disciplined valuation thresholds, and a diversified allocation that avoids speculative extremes.

**Summary**
Chance Finucane and Oxbow Advisors advocate a disciplined, long-term investment framework that prioritizes risk management over momentum chasing. With AI and semiconductor valuations presenting a 40% downside risk, the firm is rotating capital into fundamentally sound energy and precious metals positions while maintaining a defensive fixed income stance focused on short-term Treasuries. By navigating geopolitical volatility, inflation stickiness, and shifting market mechanics with patience and precision, Oxbow aims to position client portfolios for resilience and steady compounding through the anticipated 2027 market correction.
