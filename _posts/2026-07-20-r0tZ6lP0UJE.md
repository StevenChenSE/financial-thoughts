Andy dismisses short-term concerns about inventory gluts or strategic petroleum reserve (SPR) releases. He points to long-term structural issues:
*   **Peak Oil Production:** Estimates suggest peak oil production could occur between 2026 and 2030.
*   **Declining Capex:** Since 2019, 90% of upstream capital expenditure has gone toward maintaining existing production rather than finding new reserves.
*   **Falling Discovery Rates:** Annual oil discoveries are down 60% since 2010 and 90% since 1960.
*   **Rig Count Recovery:** The US oil rig count is rising, which Andy sees as a precursor to significant earnings growth for energy service companies in late 2026.

### Technical Outlook
