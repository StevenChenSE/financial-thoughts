*   **NASDAQ:** Down 0.6%

> "If we have some more bad news, we could see a pretty big move and a washout low in the markets, which could bring it right back down into this breakout and support zone from last year."

The bond market has also experienced a "bit of a bounce," leaving analysts asking critical questions about the broader trajectory:

The overarching theme is anticipation. Markets are "hinging on what is happening in the Middle East and what oil is going to do."
