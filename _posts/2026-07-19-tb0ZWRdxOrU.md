## Market Overview and Key Technicals

While it is unclear if these buyers were private institutions or central banks, the move ensured that gold did not make a new absolute intraday low, which remains anchored at the level from June 30th.

On the daily chart, the Relative Strength Index (RSI) shows a positive divergence that is holding and moving upward, suggesting easing selling pressure. However, the momentum is weakening at the upper end, forming a triangle pattern. While the RSI indicates that selling pressure is subsiding, there is currently no strong buying dynamic to drive a sustained reversal.
