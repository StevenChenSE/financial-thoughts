## A Bull Market Thesis Backed by Geopolitics and Geology

Despite weakness in precious metals like gold, base metals have continued to outperform, with copper remaining near its all-time high. Often referred to as "Dr. Copper" for its ability to mirror economic growth and stock market strength, the metal has recently shown resilience even as the Nasdaq dipped. Recent production disruptions in Chile, the world's largest copper producer, have contributed to this strength, but Algo Grande Copper CEO Enrico Guy suggests more fundamental factors are at play.

In a recent interview, Guy discussed the supply-demand imbalance, the strategic advantages of their location in Sonora, Mexico, and the latest high-grade drill results at their flagship Adelita project.

### The Copper Supply-Demand Imbalance

Guy emphasized that the current strength in copper is not merely a reaction to short-term supply chain hiccups but is indicative of a longer-term structural deficit. Analysts and mining entrepreneurs have been flagging a significant copper supply-demand imbalance for years.

Much of the future copper supply is located in remote, high-elevation, and deep deposits in the Andes, making them susceptible to operational disruptions. However, the fundamental driver remains the demand from electrification and AI infrastructure build-outs. This bullish outlook has defined Algo Grande's strategy since its inception. Listed in December 2025, the company was built on the thesis that copper would enter a prolonged bull market. Consequently, Guy is accelerating operations, deploying capital aggressively into an exploration drill program that has already exceeded 10,000 meters this year.

### Why Sonora Is Mexico’s Copper Capital

Algo Grande operates in Sonora, Mexico, a jurisdiction Guy describes as the copper capital of Mexico. Sonora produces over 90% of the country's copper concentrate and hosts world-class mines like Caridad and Buenavista.

There are several strategic reasons for choosing Sonora over traditional hubs like Chile or Peru:

*   **Mining-Friendly Jurisdiction:** Sonora has a culture that understands and supports mining, unlike other regions where resistance is common. Permits for exploration and drilling can be processed in as little as 30 days.
*   **Infrastructure:** The region boasts phenomenal infrastructure, including highways and deep-sea ports that facilitate the transport of concentrate to smelters in Asia, particularly China and Japan.
*   **Geological Expertise:** The project was brought to Algo Grande by world-renowned geologist Dr. Peter Megaw, founder of Mag Silver. Leveraging his deep understanding of the region and the high quality of the project made it a compelling opportunity compared to others globally.

Guy noted that while Mexico is often associated with gold and silver, Sonora is a critical hub for copper, sitting right on the border of Arizona and offering strategic critical mineral security for North America.

### Adelita’s Grades, Scale, and Expansion Potential

The Adelita project is currently in early-stage exploration, yet it has shown promising potential. Historically, only about 7,000 meters of drilling had been conducted on the property. Algo Grande has already surpassed this in its first six months of ownership.

Key geological highlights include:
*   **High-Grade Skarns:** The project features significant intervals up to 100 meters over 1% copper, accompanied by high-quality gold and silver credits. Average grades include 0.5 grams per ton gold and 50 to 200 grams per ton silver.
*   **Copper Equivalent:** In the skarn mineralization, grades often exceed 1.5% to 1.8% copper equivalent.
*   **Metallurgy:** Preliminary studies indicate recovery rates of 85% or higher for copper and silver.

Guy believes the current discovery is merely the tip of the iceberg. The team is drilling a 2.5-kilometer corridor to expand on the known 300-meter zone. Additionally, they have identified satellite systems, including an undrilled skarn outcrop 3 kilometers away that returned samples of over 1% copper and 1 gram per ton gold from surface samples.

### Latest Drill Results and High-Grade Copper

Algo Grande recently announced the start of its Phase Two drill program, with the second drill rig now operational. Early results from Hole Number Two are particularly encouraging. The drill encountered high-grade skarn mineralization at 100 meters, earlier than expected.

Initial visual inspection of the core revealed visible chalcocite, a copper sulfide mineral. When tested with an XRF gun, these chalcocite nuggets showed approximately 50% copper. The hole is currently targeting a depth of 700 meters, aiming to intersect a resistivity anomaly at depth that could indicate a mineralized porphyry intrusion. No previous drilling at the site had reached vertical depths beyond 400 meters.

### How Skarn and Porphyry Deposits Form

To understand the potential scale of Adelita, it is important to understand the geology. Skarns are formed through a hydrothermal process where gases and liquids travel up from a deep intrusion (a porphyry) and interact with limestone, depositing metals and creating high-grade systems.

Guy explained that skarns and porphyries are related. By understanding the skarns at the surface, the company can map the deeper porphyry intrusions that power the mineralization. Additionally, the district model includes epithermal veins, which can host significant gold and silver, similar to the neighboring Alamo Dorado mine owned by Pan American Silver, which produced 70 million ounces of silver.

### What Investors Should Look for in Drill Results

For investors evaluating drill results, Guy highlighted key indicators of success. While visual core inspection is promising, formal assays are crucial. He cited a rule of thumb from a major mining geologist: an interval of five meters at 2% copper is economic. Similarly, an interval of 10 meters at 1% copper, especially with gold and silver credits, is highly attractive.

Beyond specific intervals, investors should look for the broader geological story. Results should not only confirm high-grade mineralization but also provide clues about the deeper porphyry system and the overall scale of the district.

### Phase Two Drilling and Future Exploration

Algo Grande is currently focused on its 8,000-meter Phase Two program, which is about 10% complete. The primary goal is to expand the skarn discovery and test for deeper porphyry targets. Future programs, including a potential Phase Three, will target other prospects like the Mezquital porphyry and the Las Trancas epithermal target.

Guy remains optimistic, noting that if the project were in the US or Canada, it would likely have seen 50,000 meters of drilling already. Mexico, he argues, still offers incredible opportunities for sophisticated exploration, particularly in a region that is strategically vital for North America's critical mineral supply.

### Summary

Copper's resilience near all-time highs reflects a deepening supply-demand imbalance and growing strategic importance. Algo Grande Copper is positioning itself to capitalize on this trend through aggressive exploration at its Adelita project in Sonora, Mexico. With high-grade skarn results, a mining-friendly jurisdiction, and a complex geological system offering multiple target types, Algo Grande aims to prove the scale of a potentially transformative copper discovery.
