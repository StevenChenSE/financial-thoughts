*Veteran market analyst maintains S&P 9500 target while predicting explosive moves in precious metals*

---

The market is at an inflection point. While equities grind to fresh all-time highs, precious metals sit battered and ignored—exactly the conditions that historically precede the most explosive moves higher. That's the central thesis from David Hunter, veteran market analyst and returning guest on Capital Cosm, who spoke with host Danny on April 21st, 2026.

"The wall of worry has been rebuilt," Hunter explained. "Particularly because of what happened in Iran, you have a fear-based backdrop, and that wall of worry is the fuel for the next advance."

Hunter isn't just talking about a modest rally. His targets remain aggressive: **9,500 on the S&P 500** and new all-time highs in both gold and silver—predictions he's maintaining despite significant market volatility over the preceding weeks.

---

## The Iran Conflict: Market's Surprising Resilience

The escalating tensions with Iran dominated headlines for weeks, with bears predicting oil prices surging to $200 and economic catastrophe. The market, in classic fashion, saw through the noise.

### How Markets Priced In the Conflict

- **WTI crude peaked around $120** during the initial escalation
- **The S&P 500 sold off throughout March** as uncertainty mounted
- **Oil has since retreated to the high $80s**, signaling markets don't expect prolonged conflict
- **Equity markets hit all-time highs** just weeks after the March lows

Hunter attributes this counterintuitive behavior to Trump's strategic calculus: "You had everybody coming out of the woodwork talking bearish... and the market in its typical wisdom looked ahead and said no, that's not the case."

### What's Really Driving the Resolution

According to Hunter, President Trump faces midterm election pressures that make extended conflict politically untenable:

- **Oil at significantly higher prices** would alienate voters already frustrated
- **The Iranian military has sustained substantial damage**, weakening negotiating position
- **Defections within Iranian military leadership** suggest the regime sees the writing on the wall
- **Trump's preferred outcome**: Negotiated settlement removing enriched uranium from Iran

"The market's nervous about whether as soon as this negotiation doesn't go well, we're going to see bombs dropping again," Hunter noted. "But I suspect it's going to be over pretty quickly here."

## The S&P Meltup Thesis: Final Leg to 9,500

Hunter has maintained his **9,500 S&P target** since October 2025, and the recent market action has only strengthened his conviction.

### The Mathematics of the Meltup

| Period | S&P 500 Performance |
|--------|-------------------|
| 2023 | +24% |
| 2024 | +24% |
| 2025 | +16% |
| 2026 YTD (as of April 21) | +3.3% |

While some point to this deceleration as evidence of a maturing top, Hunter sees a different pattern: **successively steeper legs in the final phase of a secular bull market**.

"If you look at the move out of last April, when we had the tariff sell-off, you went up 41% in the S&P between April 2025 and October 2025," Hunter explained. "Each leg has gotten steeper from the previous leg. I think we have just entered the final steepest leg."

### Projected Move Calculation

- **Recent lows**: Approximately 6,200
- **Target**: 9,500
- **Required gain**: ~53% from bottom
- **Timeframe**: Summer 2026

"If we did 41% in six months last time, you've got that steeper leg that I think is the final leg," Hunter stated. "Pretty good run, but I think we can do it."

---

## The 44-Year Secular Bull Market's Grand Finale

Hunter frames the current action within the context of a **secular bull market that began in 1982**—spanning four decades of generally rising prices.

### Why This Isn't Your Typical Midyear Pullback

The historical pattern for midterm election years suggests weakness:

- **2022**: S&P corrected from 4,700 to 3,600
- **2018**: S&P corrected from 2,800 to 2,500

However, Hunter believes this year breaks the pattern: "We've rebuilt the wall of worry. You have a fear-based backdrop, and that wall of worry is the fuel for the next advance. That's more important than whether historically we see midterms perform poorly."

### The Global "Bus" Scenario Remains Intact

Hunter's controversial "global bus" thesis—predicting an eventual worldwide economic bust—remains unchanged despite recent geopolitical turbulence:

> "What went on in Iran just creates more fragility in the system. You've got not so much in the US, although some, because there's half the economy just getting by. It doesn't help them to have gas prices up a dollar. However, the real problem is in Asia and in Europe. They've not only got a big price increase, but they also have availability issues."

The scenario for how this plays out:

- **If negotiated settlement occurs**: Oil drops to $70 quickly, market rallies on relief
- **If bombing resumes**: Oil back above $100, stocks sell off sharply
- **Hunter's base case**: Resolution comes quickly, economy continues toward eventual bust

## Oil: From $120 to the $60s?

The oil market's reaction to the Iran situation has been equally instructive.

### Current Oil Dynamics

- **Recent high**: ~$120 (WTI crude)
- **Current price**: ~$88
- **Hunter's target if resolved**: **Down to $70 quickly**, then potentially the $60s

### Why Oil Could Plunge Despite Recent Supply Disruptions

Hunter challenges the prevailing narrative about lasting supply damage:

- **Pre-conflict surplus**: Oil had dropped into the mid-$50s before the Iran situation
- **Market memory**: Venezuela's production issues are already factored in
- **Forward-looking nature**: Markets anticipate resolution, not prolonged shortage
- **European and Asian inventory**: Will take time to rebuild, but underlying supply exists

"I think traders are overly bearish on oil prices in terms of what it's going to do," Hunter observed. "You're going to find once this gets resolved, we're going to go back to a situation where Europe's going to take some time to get straightened out, Asia's probably going to take time to get their inventory back up. But I think the markets, being forward-looking, are going to move oil down pretty quickly."

## Gold and Silver: The Contrarian Opportunity

This is where Hunter's thesis becomes most provocative—and most compelling for precious metals investors.

### Metals as "Risk On" Trades

Counterintuitively, gold and silver have been trading alongside equities rather than as crisis hedges:

- **January 2026**: Massive runup in metals
- **February 2026**: Sharp selloff following the initial rally
- **March 2026**: Rolled over and made lower lows when the Iran war began
- **Current**: Building back, but more damaged than equities

"Gold and silver are basically risk-on trades right now," Hunter explained. "They track really the stock market more than they track anything else at that point because they were both risk-off trades."

### The Case for New All-Time Highs

Hunter's prediction is unequivocal: **new highs in both gold and silver this quarter—in Q2 2026**.

> "Whereas equity markets have gone to new highs, I think there was more damage done to the metals, and it's taken more—investors are still pretty skeptical about where it's going. So I'm... I think you're going to see new highs in both gold and silver this quarter."

### Silver Mining Stock Targets

Hunter provided specific price targets for silver-focused ETFs:

- **GDX (large-cap gold miners)**: 180
- **GDXJ (junior gold miners)**: 250
- **SIL (large-cap silver miners)**: 220
- **SILJ (junior silver miners)**: **90**

"That's a triple in the junior miners," Hunter noted. "These are aggressive moves if they happen. And then along with the stock market, you've got big downside on the other side of this—so this is the end of a move, not the beginning of anything."

### Why Miners Haven't Been Crushed by Higher Oil

Energy costs are a significant input for mining operations, yet the mining stocks have held their own relative to spot metals prices. Hunter sees disciplined management as the explanation:

- **Past cycles**: Mining companies engaged in reckless M&A, buying assets at cycle highs
- **Current cycle**: "They've shown much more discipline and are much more focused on their operations"
- **Contract dynamics**: While some sales are hedged, rising spot prices have more than offset cost increases

## The Bond Market: A 4.3% Ceiling

The 10-year Treasury yield has repeatedly tested the 4.3% level without breaking through—a pattern that Hunter interprets as a **major multi-year top**.

### Hunter's Bond Thesis

- **Current range**: Low 4% to 4.3%
- **Interpretation**: This represents a 2.5-3 year top in rates
- **Direction**: Expecting a break to the downside, not upside

The timing coincides with Fed Chair confirmation hearings for Kevin Moore, adding political dimension to an already complex picture. Hunter's view: the structural case for higher rates is exhausted, and the next meaningful move in yields is lower.

## The Sentiment Paradox

Looking at market mood indicators, Hunter sees both warning signs and fuel for further gains.

### What the Market Mood Meter Shows

- **Late March 2026**: Extreme fear at recent lows
- **Current**: Sentiment has swung dramatically positive
- **Historical parallel**: Similar spike occurred during April 2025 tariff selloff, followed by year-end consolidation

"From the bottom in March, you spiked up sentiment very quickly," Hunter observed. "But it doesn't mean you quickly sell at the top of that sentiment indicator because there's still probably several months where people have to act on their bullishness."

This mirrors 2025's pattern: extreme greed followed by correction, yet the market continued higher at a decelerating pace. Hunter expects similar dynamics ahead—perhaps one more significant push before the inevitable bust.

## Key Takeaways

1. **The S&P 500 is in its final meltup leg toward 9,500**—a 50%+ move from recent lows that Hunter expects to complete by summer 2026

2. **Iran will resolve quickly**, either through negotiated settlement or limited additional military action—oil's relief rally to $70+ will be short-lived as markets price in resolution

3. **Gold and silver will make new all-time highs this quarter**, despite recent weakness and investor skepticism—the wall of worry provides the fuel for explosive moves

4. **Silver miners could triple from current levels**—junior silver miners (SILJ) targeted at 90, with Hunter's calls representing the end of a major move, not the beginning

5. **The 44-year secular bull market is nearing its end**—this final leg is the steepest, driven by sentiment extremes and built-in fragility in the global economy, setting up Hunter's eventual "global bus" scenario

> *"I view it from a contrarian standpoint—if sentiment's pretty bearish, it's fuel for the next advance, and I think it's going to be a big advance. And because I do think we're coming to the end of what will probably turn out to be a 44-year secular bull market that started back in 1982."*
