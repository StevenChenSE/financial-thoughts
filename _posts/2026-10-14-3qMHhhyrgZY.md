## What Is a Gold IRA?

While commonly referred to as a "Gold IRA," the correct terminology is a **self-directed physical precious metals IRA**. It is a registered retirement account that allows the account holder to own physical bullion directly.

Unlike traditional IRAs that typically hold stocks, bonds, and mutual funds (often in a 60/40 or 70/30 allocation), a self-directed precious metals IRA permits the ownership of tangible assets. These can include:
*   **Metals:** Gold, silver, and platinum.
*   **Formats:** Coins and bars of various sizes.
*   **Sourcing:** Metals can be sourced from anywhere, provided they meet IRS purity requirements.

Proponents of this strategy argue that physical bullion offers unique benefits, including:
*   **No Counterparty Risk:** Unlike ETFs or mining stocks, physical metal does not rely on a third party’s promise to pay.
*   **Currency Hedge:** It acts as a hedge against currency devaluation and inflation.
*   **Portfolio Diversification:** It is often uncorrelated or counter-cyclical to traditional stock and bond markets.

## Who Is Eligible?

Almost any holder of a registered retirement account is eligible to open a self-directed precious metals IRA. The source of the funds determines the specific rules regarding transfers.

### Individual Retirement Accounts (IRAs)
If you already have an IRA, you can transfer funds at any time. Eligible account types include:
*   Traditional IRAs
*   Roth IRAs
*   Inherited IRAs
*   Self-Employed Plans (SEPs, SIMPLE IRAs)

### Employer-Sponsored Plans (401k, 403b, TSP)
For funds held in employer plans such as 401(k)s, 403(b)s, 457s, or the Thrift Savings Plan (TSP), the rules depend on your employment status:

1.  **Old/Former Employer Plans:** If you have an account from a previous job, you can roll it over into a self-directed IRA at any time, regardless of your age. Many investors forget these accounts exist, only to find significant nest eggs years later.
2.  **Current Employer Plans:** To roll over funds from a current employer’s plan, you must be **older than 59 and a half**. This is known as an "active service rollover." If you are under this age, you generally cannot move funds from your current employer's plan without penalties or restrictions.

## The Three-Party Structure

Setting up a precious metals IRA involves a triangle of three distinct entities. You cannot simply call your standard broker (like Fidelity or Vanguard) and buy physical gold for your IRA; they do not offer this service.

### 1. The Precious Metals Dealer/Broker
This is your primary point of contact and advisor. In this example, **Noble Gold** serves as the full-service dealer. Their role includes:
*   Opening the IRA and custodial accounts.
*   Advising on portfolio allocation.
*   Purchasing the physical metal.
*   Delivering and "inducting" the metal into the depository.
*   Providing a buyback guarantee.

### 2. The Custodian
The custodian is the financial institution that maintains the official IRA paperwork and holds the cash or assets in a tax-advantaged structure. While major firms like Fidelity and Schwab are custodians for standard IRAs, only a handful of specialized custodians handle self-directed precious metals IRAs. **Equity Trust Company** is a prominent example, having operated for over 50 years.

### 3. The Depository
Because you are buying a tangible asset, it must be stored in a secure, insured facility in your name. This is called a **segregated vaulted account**. Think of it as a safety deposit box linked to your IRA account number. The metal is held here to maintain the tax-deferred status of the IRA. Withdrawing the metal to your home would trigger taxes and potential penalties.

## The Step-by-Step Process

The process is streamlined and typically completed within two weeks.

1.  **Open the Account:** Contact the dealer (e.g., Noble Gold) to determine your existing account type. They will send paperwork to open a self-directed IRA with the specialized custodian (like Equity Trust). This takes about one business day.
2.  **Transfer Funds:** The dealer facilitates the transfer of funds from your existing IRA or employer plan into the new self-directed IRA. They handle the paperwork and communication with your previous custodian to ensure a direct rollover, avoiding tax events.
3.  **Purchase and Induct Metal:** Work with your advisor to select the specific coins and bars you wish to own. The dealer purchases the metal and delivers it to the depository.
