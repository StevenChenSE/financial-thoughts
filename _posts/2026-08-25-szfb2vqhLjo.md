Crude oil has been a point of significant divergence in market sentiment. Technically, Horwitz points to the concept of backwardation in the oil market, where the front-month contract is priced higher than future contracts due to near-term fear premiums. With December oil trading at $81 and August 2027 oil at $72, he expects the fear premium to dissipate.
