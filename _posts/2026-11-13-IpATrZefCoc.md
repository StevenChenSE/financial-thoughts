## Silver Elephant Mining: Legal Victory in Bolivia

The conversation pivoted to a major development for Silver Elephant Mining Corp. (ELF). The company’s flagship project, Pulkayo in Bolivia, faced significant legal challenges after a mining production contract was canceled by the Bolivian government's mining ministry subsidiary, Kamiball.

Lee described the situation as being akin to "squatters" taking over their project. After nearly two years of litigation in both Bolivia and Canada, the Bolivian Supreme Court issued a ruling that vindicated Silver Elephant. Key findings included:
1.  **Procedural Failure:** Kamiball failed to consider all permits and authorizations issued to Silver Elephant.
2.  **Invalid Cancellation:** There was no legal basis for canceling the mining production contract, and proper procedures for defense were not followed.

This ruling effectively nullifies the cancellation resolution from 2024, restoring the validity of the original contract signed in 2019. Lee confirmed that the squatters have vacated the premises, allowing the company to move forward.
