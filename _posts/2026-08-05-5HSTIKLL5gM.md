## Why Oil Offers More Potential Than AI or Tech Right Now

In a market increasingly fixated on artificial intelligence and technology stocks, Clive Maund argues that the oil sector presents a far more compelling investment opportunity. With global supply constraints intensifying and inventories nearing critical lows, Maund believes that oil prices are set for a significant upward move. This analysis focuses on technical chart patterns for Brent crude and the United States Oil Fund (USO), highlighting a bullish setup that could see prices surge well beyond recent highs.

## Geopolitical Risks Driving Supply Constraints

The foundation of this bullish outlook lies in a series of escalating geopolitical events that threaten global oil supply. Maund outlines several critical factors:

- **Middle East Tensions:** The collapse of the Iran deal and ongoing threats of conflict could severely disrupt production.
- **Red Sea Disruptions:** Houthi attacks have forced the closure of key shipping routes, compelling Saudi Arabia to reroute exports across the Arabian Peninsula, reducing efficiency and capacity.
- **Russian Production Cuts:** Ukrainian strikes have reportedly crippled 25–30% of Russian oil production capacity.
- **Inventory Depletion:** Strategic reserves, including the Cushing inventory in the U.S., have been drawn down to near "tank bottom," eliminating the buffer that previously softened price spikes.
- **Infrastructure Vulnerability:** Potential attacks on Gulf desalination plants could render key oil-producing regions uninhabitable, risking up to a 40% reduction in global supply.

These factors, combined with unexplained fires at refineries worldwide, create a scenario where supply is increasingly unable to meet demand. From an investment standpoint, Maund views this as a catalyst for a sustained price rally.

## Technical Analysis: Brent Crude

Maund’s technical analysis of Brent crude focuses on two timeframes: the two-year chart and the six-month chart.

On the two-year chart, Brent crude previously formed a double bottom pattern before breaking out in the spring. After rallying, the price pulled back to test support near the neckline of that pattern. Maund notes that despite geopolitical tensions, oil prices had briefly returned to pre-February levels—a level he considers unsustainable given current supply risks.

The six-month chart reveals a more detailed picture. Following a heavy correction to support in early July, Brent crude reversed sharply. Maund identifies this move as the formation of a head and shoulders bottom pattern. The left shoulder formed during the initial pullback, the head marked the July low, and the price is now consolidating to form the right shoulder. A breakout above the neckline resistance—aligned with the prior double top—could propel prices toward $120, $150, or even $200 per barrel.

Key technical indicators support this view:
- Moving averages are in bullish alignment.
- The 200-day moving average remains rising.
- The MACD shows oversold conditions turning bullish.

## Technical Analysis: United States Oil Fund (USO)

The United States Oil Fund (USO), an ETF that tracks WTI crude oil prices, mirrors the technical setup of Brent crude.

On the two-year chart, USO experienced a strong rally in the spring on high volume, followed by a pullback on significantly lighter volume. This volume pattern suggests the spring move was an impulse wave in the direction of the primary trend. Additionally, the accumulation line remained elevated during the pullback, indicating continued buying interest by institutional investors.

The six-month chart shows USO correcting to its rising 200-day moving average in early July before reversing sharply. Like Brent crude, USO appears to be forming a head and shoulders consolidation pattern. A breakout above the spring highs would confirm the continuation of the uptrend.
