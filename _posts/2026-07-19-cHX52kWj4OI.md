In a candid interview on *Capital*, renowned trend forecaster and publisher of *The Trends Journal*, Gerald Celente, shared his deeply concerning outlook for the future. Celente argues that the current enthusiasm for artificial intelligence mirrors the dot-com bubble of the late 1990s. He points out that major tech stocks, including IBM, Oracle, and Asian markets, have already seen significant corrections. He highlights the rapid rise of Chinese AI models like DeepSeek, which caused a massive one-day drop in Nvidia’s stock value, as evidence that China is leading the world in AI efficiency and adoption.

"The AI trend has just been born," Celente explained. "You don't invest all your dough in the companies that are infants." He noted that the Shiller PE ratio remains dangerously high at around 40, far above the historical average of 17, indicating that the market is severely overvalued. He predicts a "dot-com 2.0" crash that will trigger a global equity market collapse, leaving retail investors with significant losses while elite institutions are bailed out.

Celente described the current political climate as increasingly fascist, defined by the merger of state and corporate powers.
