## A Forecast for Economic Collapse and Global Instability

Trends Journal publisher Gerald Celente recently joined Capital host Danny for an in-depth discussion on the critical geopolitical and economic trends shaping 2026. With 46 years of forecasting experience, Celente expressed unprecedented concern about the future of life on Earth, highlighting a convergence of market crashes, escalating wars, and the rise of corporate-state fascism.

## The Impending AI Bubble Burst

Celente identified a "dot-com bust 2.0" as a primary trend for 2026, driven by the overvaluation of artificial intelligence stocks. He pointed to significant declines in major tech stocks like SpaceX, IBM, and Oracle, noting that the Shiller PE ratio stands at approximately 40.1, far above the sustainable level of 17.

The discussion highlighted the disparity between American and Chinese AI development. While the U.S. builds thousands of energy-intensive data centers facing local opposition, China is leveraging its massive population and educational focus on high-tech to lead the world in AI efficiency. Celente cited the impact of China's Deep Seek AI, which caused a massive drop in Nvidia's stock value, as evidence that the world is shifting toward more efficient, less expensive Chinese AI solutions.

## Geopolitical Escalation and False Flags

The interview covered the ongoing conflicts in Ukraine and the Middle East. Celente criticized the narrative surrounding the Ukraine war, referencing the 2014 overthrow of the democratically elected government of Viktor Yanukovych, which he attributed to U.S. involvement via Victoria Nuland and Jeffrey Pyatt. He argued that the war is being prolonged to drain resources and destabilize Russia.

Regarding the conflict with Iran, Celente dismissed claims by Donald Trump and Benjamin Netanyahu that Iran was on the verge of acquiring nuclear weapons or being militarily defeated. He predicted that the war will escalate further during the summer, potentially triggered by a false flag event designed to unite public opinion against Iran, similar to the justification used for entering World War II after Pearl Harbor or the wars following 9/11.

## The Rise of Corporate Fascism

Celente described the current political landscape as a form of fascism, defined by the merger of state and corporate powers. He pointed to the dominance of private equity groups like BlackRock, Vanguard, and State Street, which collectively own 88% of the S&P 500. He argued that these entities are bailing out their own interests while ordinary citizens face financial ruin.

Key points regarding this shift include:
*   **Bailouts vs. Austerity:** While the Levy Institute estimates $29 trillion has been used to bail out banks and corporations, individuals have lost homes and savings.
*   **Debt Crisis:** The real U.S. debt, including unfunded liabilities like Social Security, exceeds $126 trillion.
*   **Digital Currency:** Celente warned of the introduction of a "fake currency" or digital trash to manage the debt crisis, moving away from the traditional dollar.

## Economic Hardship and the Gen Z Revolution

The conversation addressed the severe economic pressure on the general population. Celente noted that first-time homebuyer rates have dropped from 48% to 28%, with many young people forced to live with parents due to unaffordable rents and mortgage costs. He attributed this to inflation driven by war spending and monetary policies since 2020, which saw home prices rise 53% and automobile prices rise 40%.

This economic disenfranchisement is fueling a "Gen Z revolution" globally. Celente cited examples from India, where a "Cockroach Party" gained millions of followers after a cabinet member insulted young people, and movements in Indonesia and Nepal where youth are rising against tycoons and corrupt governments. He argued that the middle class has been decimated by big-box retailers and corporate consolidation, leaving the majority as "plantation workers of Slandia."

## Gold, Silver, and the Death of the Dollar

Despite current price declines, Celente remains bullish on gold and silver in the long term. He explained that recent drops are due to countries like Turkey, Russia, and Mexico selling gold to cover debt and energy costs, as well as private equity groups liquidating assets to cover losses in the AI sector.

However, he predicted that when the stock market crashes and the greatest depression hits, gold prices will spike. He foresees the "death of the dollar" as BRICS nations and over 50 other countries move away from U.S. hegemony. He believes new global currencies will likely be backed by gold, restoring value to precious metals.

## A Call for a Renaissance

Concluding the interview, Celente reflected on the cultural decay of America, contrasting the style and spirit of the mid-20th century with the current state of affairs. He referenced his book, *What Zizzy Gave Honeyboy*, as a tribute to the soul of America. He argued that a peaceful revolution is necessary, driven by an "irate, tireless minority," to restore freedom and dismantle the corporate-state power structure. He emphasized that true change will only occur when the status quo becomes too uncomfortable to maintain, likely catalyzed by the impending collapse of Wall Street.
