## The Two-Engine Pull for Western Gold

Yeung argues that this migration sets the stage for a repeat of the bull market dynamics seen in 2023 and 2024. Back then, high gold prices in China created an arbitrage opportunity that pulled physical gold from Western exchanges like the LBMA and COMEX, driving the price from $1,800 to over $3,000.

Now, the mechanism is expanding. As liquidity floods into the SHFE, arbitrage between the SGE and SHFE will widen. Initially, this will involve moving domestic Chinese gold. However, as demand intensifies, the SHFE may begin importing physical gold from the West, just as the SGE did previously. This creates a "two-engine" scenario where both Chinese exchanges are pulling physical supply from Western vaults, providing substantial upward pressure on COMEX and LBMA pricing. Yeung notes that the arbitrage between Shanghai and New York has already returned to positive territory, coinciding with the upcoming deadline.
