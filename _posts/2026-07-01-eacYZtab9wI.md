## From Retail Fad to Institutional Cornerstone: Bitcoin's Transformation

In a recent interview, Binance Co-CEO Richard Tang sat down to discuss the evolving cryptocurrency landscape, the transformation of Bitcoin from a retail-dominated asset to an institutional powerhouse, and what the world's largest crypto exchange has planned for the remainder of 2026 and beyond.

The conversation began with an examination of Bitcoin's fundamental shift in investor composition. Tang acknowledged that the current market cycle has seen dramatically increased institutional participation compared to previous years. "In past cycles, it was mainly a retail play," Tang explained. "The institutions, the corporates, the regulators were sitting on the sideline. This cycle definitely sees a lot more institutional interest."

This transformation has been catalyzed by several factors, most notably the introduction of Bitcoin ETFs that provide new forms of access to the asset class. "All those are very exciting, and you have seen a slight maturity in terms of Bitcoin and crypto," Tang noted. "It has now become more and more mainstream into the financial services infrastructure."

However, Tang remains a believer in the four-year cycle theory, attributing it to the halving events and Bitcoin's inherent scarcity. "I still believe in a four-year cycle because with the halving episode and scarcity coming into play on a four-year basis, I do believe that is still at play," he stated. "The cost of mining is still important."

## The Evolution of Binance: From 170 Million to 320 Million Users

The user base evolution at Binance tells a compelling story of the broader crypto market's maturation. Two years ago, the exchange served approximately 170 million customers. Today, that number has nearly doubled to 320 million, with significant changes in user composition.

"What's changed is institutions, corporates, and in many instances sovereigns are coming in," Tang highlighted. "They have different investment appetites, different investment thesis horizons, and that adds diversity to different areas of investments."

This increased diversity has created a more robust and healthier marketplace. Notably, while retail investors have been more cautious in this cycle, institutions and "smart money" have been adding to their portfolios with a long-term outlook. "They have a very robust outlook of this particular asset class and they tend to invest for very long term in terms of returns," Tang observed.

## Capital Rotation: A Temporary Phenomenon?

The interview addressed the recent price correction in Bitcoin—from its late 2024 highs in October down to around $60,000 by June—amid reports of $4 billion in ETF outflows since mid-May. Some analysts, including Michael Saylor, characterized this as "capital rotation, not a Bitcoin impairment," suggesting funds were temporarily flowing into tech as the AI buildout captured attention.

Tang agreed with this assessment, viewing the rotation as part of natural market dynamics. "It happens every time an asset class becomes hot or popular—you tend to see funds flow into that asset class," he explained. "This is something that's quite natural, and we have Binance Research that shows the correlation. Every time S&P, NASDAQ reach all-time highs, it probably takes away some liquidity from the Bitcoin market."

Tang also noted that some of this correction may be attributable to typical market cycles, but emphasized that investors should focus on fundamental narratives rather than short-term fluctuations.

## The Blockchain and AI Convergence

A significant portion of the discussion focused on how blockchain technology and artificial intelligence are becoming complementary forces in financial services.

"AI, in a nutshell, makes things much more efficient and accurate," Tang explained. "Blockchain, by its nature, is immutable. These are really complimentary assets."

The convergence is particularly powerful in financial services middle and back office operations. Tang described how traditional clearing, settlement, and reconciliation work—currently requiring T+2 settlement—could be transformed through blockchain and AI. "In blockchain, we use atomic settlement, which is instantaneous, at a fraction of the price compared to traditional financial services."

The same applies to cross-border payments. "If you try to remit money cross-border traditionally, it takes two days at very high cost," Tang noted. "Stablecoins versus that represent a very important showcase of how the financial services landscape can transform."

## Binance's Transformation into a Financial Super App

Perhaps the most striking revelation was Binance's evolution beyond a cryptocurrency exchange into a comprehensive financial services platform. The company now offers access to stocks, ETFs, tokenized assets, payments, and private market opportunities including pre-IPO perpetuals.

"We have 320 million customers globally," Tang said. "Not many financial apps in the world can lay claim to that. Our ambition is to expand to stocks, ETF, tokenized assets, payments, private market opportunities that are now available on our platform."

The exchange has made significant inroads with products like SpaceX pre-IPO perpetuals, accounting for 60% of trading in this product—approximately $3 billion in volume. On the first day of SpaceX's IPO, trading volume on Binance's platform reached nearly $6 billion.

The demographic data from Binance's stock trading function reveals fascinating trends: 80% of users come from emerging markets, with a quarter of them under 25 years old. Many of these users are locked out of traditional markets due to structural barriers. "We provide access to many people that previously have no access," Tang emphasized. "If you look at globally, only about 630 million stock trading accounts are available—that's about 11% of the global population."

## The Rise of Tokenized Securities: B-Stocks

Binance's B-Stocks product, launched in June 2025, represents the exchange's push into tokenized securities. These are one-to-one tokenized versions of US securities that trade 24/7.

Nearly 50% of B-Stocks trading occurs outside US trading hours, demonstrating the demand for around-the-clock access. The benefits extend beyond trading hours: "Once an asset is on-chain, you can do a lot more things with it," Tang explained. "You can transfer quite easily for collateral purposes, for earn products. It's very cumbersome in traditional markets, but once it's on-chain, you can do yield enhancement, collateral management, and transfers on a much easier basis."

Tang also addressed concerns about backing, confirming that underlying assets are backed one-to-one on the Binance Reserve.

## MiCA in Europe: Binance's Commitment Remains Strong

The interview addressed recent developments regarding Binance's MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation) license application in Europe. Reports indicated that Binance withdrew its application from Greece, which had reportedly been on track for approval before an unexpected reversal.

"We withdraw the application from Greece, which is very unfortunate," Tang acknowledged. "We were given the understanding that our application was fully compliant and would be approved until the 11th hour, when there appears to be a change of heart that obviously caught us by surprise."

Crucially, Tang emphasized that Binance is not leaving Europe. "We remain very committed to Europe. We are pursuing authorization with another EU member state." The company is prioritizing user protection and compliance with European laws while working tirelessly with regulators across the bloc.

Binance positions itself as the most regulated entity globally, with comprehensive oversight by the Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) financial services regulatory authority. "Our activities are regulated on an end-to-end basis—listings, AML, KYC, transaction monitoring, wallet management, risk management, and governance," Tang noted. "Nobody else can lay claim to that."

The company allocates approximately 25% of its total headcount to compliance functions, demonstrating its commitment to regulatory standards.

## The US Market: Awaiting Regulatory Clarity

Regarding potential reentry into the US market, Tang noted that discussions are ongoing but complex. The exchange is awaiting the passing of the Clarity Act, which would provide much greater regulatory clarity for the cryptocurrency industry.

"US is extremely important as a marketplace, but it's also very complex," Tang explained. "The landscape is very diverse—diverse not only in terms of participants but also in the interpretation of laws and requirements at federal versus state levels."

Tang also noted that existing market participants are lobbying against Binance's potential entrance, as the exchange's dynamic marketplace and lower fees would create significant competition. "US users will benefit greatly with our entrance, but existing players will suffer in terms of fee compression when consumers get much more choice and can trade at much lower costs."

## Addressing False Allegations: Legal Action Taken

The interview touched on recent Wall Street Journal reports claiming Iran moved billions through Binance. Tang was emphatic in refuting these claims.

"We have taken out lawsuits against them. We will continue to pursue aggressively to make sure that all these falsehood stops," Tang stated. "There's proper justice for all this falsehood against us."

Binance has provided detailed fund tracking demonstrating these allegations are false to regulators and law enforcement agencies. In the past year alone, the exchange processed 71,000 law enforcement requests and received commendation letters from agencies in jurisdictions where it isn't even licensed.

The exposure to illicit activity has dropped by 97% from early 2024 to mid-2025, and Binance's metrics compare favorably to the next six largest exchanges.

## Looking Ahead to 2030: The Three Billion User Ambition

When asked about Binance's evolution into a fintech company rather than merely a crypto company, Tang clarified the vision.

"I like to think of ourselves as more than just a fintech company," he said. "We are part of financial infrastructure going forward. We are part of a financial super app. I want us to be a very important part of the financial ecosystem."

Looking ahead to 2030, Tang expressed ambitious goals: "Our user pool has grown to closer to three billion—that's our ambition. Our asset product suite is much more comprehensive. We are in a lot more jurisdictions."

He anticipates that AI and agentic trading will become major components of the ecosystem, with blockchain infrastructure becoming integral to daily life.

## Singapore's Economic Transformation: Lessons in Long-Term Planning

As a Singaporean who previously worked at the Monetary Authority of Singapore, Tang offered insights into his home country's remarkable transformation from a rural fishing and agricultural community to a global commercial and financial center in just a few decades.

"What you see today is not by chance," Tang reflected. "Singapore is able to take a very long-term view of things, become very strategic, and invest into the long term."

The success story involves much more than tax incentives. Tang emphasized the importance of ecosystem development—easing business setup, attracting talent pools, building infrastructure, and creating a vibrant, livable city with international schools and recreational facilities.

When asked about the balance between government planning and free markets, Tang offered nuanced perspectives. "For small states like Singapore and UAE, the development strategy is very different from large states with big domestic markets," he explained. "Small states must be nimble and agile to compete. They need to support innovation and attract the right talent, who bring new ideas, business activity, and investments."

He expressed concern that some large economies are implementing policies that force talent out to the benefit of competitors, with long-term repercussions for innovation and growth.

## Conclusion

The interview with Richard Tang revealed a Binance that has evolved far beyond its cryptocurrency exchange origins into a global financial super app with 320 million users and ambitions to reach three billion. The platform is positioning itself at the intersection of blockchain technology and artificial intelligence, offering traditional financial products alongside innovative crypto services.

As the cryptocurrency industry continues its institutional maturation, Binance's strategy reflects the broader trend: integrating traditional finance with blockchain-native capabilities, expanding access to underserved populations globally, and building the infrastructure for a more efficient, 24/7 financial system.
