## The AI Trade Cracks as Markets Rotate

Gareth Soloway, president of Verified Investing, warns that the semiconductor trade is cracking due to forward-looking market dynamics and slowing demand signals. While semiconductors may eventually face a 75% correction, Soloway sees short-term bounce opportunities and a broader rotation of capital into mega-caps, gold, and Bitcoin.

## Semiconductor Sector Analysis

The "memory trade" and chip sector are showing weakness as markets look 12 months ahead. Soloway notes that new factories are being built and companies are trying to maximize existing memory usage due to high prices. Micron has dropped 36% from its all-time high, and Soloway expects semiconductors to eventually see downside of up to 75%, similar to previous tech bubbles.

However, he is currently long some semiconductors for a short-term bounce, citing support zones and Fibonacci retraces. He bought Seagate Technologies (STX) and is looking at names like Marvell (MRVL) for technical bounces. Soloway warns that extreme analyst optimism and ridiculous price targets are often top signals, citing the recent hype around Nvidia, Micron, and SpaceX as examples of market tops.

## Market Rotation and Key Levels

Soloway describes a rotation of capital out of semiconductors and into mega-cap stocks like Apple, Microsoft, and Meta. Apple is acting as a cushion for the NASDAQ, but Soloway expects it to eventually fall as well. He highlights Oracle (ORCL) as an attractive buy due to extreme bearish sentiment and technical support.

Key levels to watch include:
*   **S&P 500:** 7,330 is the critical support line. A break below could lead to 7,000 and potentially 6,300.
*   **NASDAQ:** 25,000 is the key support. A breakdown could lead to a quick flush to 24,000.
*   **Apple:** Facing massive resistance, expected to correct after the semi bounce.

## Federal Reserve and Oil Prices

Soloway expects at most one rate hike this year, with cuts possible in 2027 due to expected economic weakness. He believes political pressures will limit the Fed's ability to hike rates aggressively.

Regarding oil, Soloway sees a short-term top around $87 per barrel and plans to short it there. He expects oil to drop back to the $60s by the midterms due to political pressures, despite recent geopolitical tensions. He believes a sustained move above $100 is unlikely this year.

## Gold and Bitcoin Outlook

Gold is currently trading around $4,000, and Soloway expects a final flush down to $3,500 before a long-term rally. He sees a wedge pattern forming, which could lead to an explosive move in either direction. He is a long-term bull on gold due to fiscal concerns.

Bitcoin is forming an inverse head-and-shoulders pattern, suggesting a potential rally to $71,000-$72,000. Soloway sees rotation of capital back into Bitcoin and gold as the stock market cools. Key support for Bitcoin is $58,000, with a breakdown potentially leading to $50,000.

## Conclusion

Soloway advises traders to watch key technical levels and be prepared for a rotation of capital. While semiconductors face a significant long-term correction, short-term opportunities exist in bounces and in other asset classes like mega-caps, gold, and Bitcoin. Investors should remain cautious of market tops signaled by extreme analyst optimism and retail hype.
