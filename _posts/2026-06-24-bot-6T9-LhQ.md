## Veteran Investor Warns We're in the Early Innings of a Market Collapse as Geopolitical Tensions Reach Critical Levels

The financial markets are sending distress signals, and one veteran investor believes the worst is still ahead. Bob Moriarty, publisher of 321gold.com, recently appeared on Capital Cosm to discuss the current state of the markets and the escalating geopolitical situation that could trigger a global economic collapse.

"This is the weirdest time frame for the last four months that I've ever seen," Moriarty stated during the June 23rd, 2026 interview. "If you're not confused, you're not paying attention."

## A Perfect Storm Across All Asset Classes

The current market conditions are alarming investors worldwide. Nearly every major asset class is experiencing significant declines simultaneously:

- **Oil prices** continue to fall, currently trading around $73 per barrel
- **Gold and silver** are both crashing, with silver falling below key support levels at $60
- **Bonds** are down across the board
- **Stock markets** are closing in the red

The only notable exception is the U.S. Dollar Index (DXY), which has strengthened as investors seek safety in cash positions.

"This is not an early warning signal," Moriarty warned. "I think it's a late incoming warning signal. We are in the crash we've been talking about for a long time. AI is teetering on the head of a pin and it's about to crash. The stock market is more overblown than it's ever been."

## The Geopolitical Powder Keg

Beyond traditional market concerns, Moriarty highlighted dangerous geopolitical dynamics that could accelerate economic deterioration. The discussion touched on the complex web of international conflicts involving multiple nuclear-capable powers.

### The Middle East Situation

According to Moriarty, three major players are driving the Middle East conflict: Israel, the United States, and Iran. The situation has become increasingly complicated, with claims that Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu possesses leverage over former President Donald Trump through the Epstein files.

"Donald Trump is mentioned in the Epstein files 38,000 times," Moriarty alleged. "Trump is fighting tooth and nail for the Epstein files not to be released."

This dynamic has effectively trapped the United States in what Moriarty characterizes as "Israel's war," despite Trump's stated desire to avoid foreign entanglements.

### The Greater Israel Project

Moriarty directed viewers to research what he called the "Greater Israel Project," which allegedly involves territorial ambitions spanning from Egypt to Iraq. While most regional nations would oppose such a plan, Moriarty noted that "for 80 years, Israel's gotten away with murder, and the entire world now understands exactly what they're facing."

### Russia and NATO: A Dangerous Escalation

Perhaps most alarming is the situation developing between Russia and NATO. The United Kingdom has supplied Ukraine with Storm Shadow long-range missiles, which have been fired into Russian territory with significant destructive effect.

"This is not just an issue of the UK supplying the missiles," Moriarty explained. "It's an issue of they supply the missiles and somebody supplies the satellite intelligence and somebody supplies the targeting information and somebody monitors it. Ukraine is not at war with Russia. NATO and the US are at war with Russia."

Russian President Vladimir Putin has reportedly issued a direct warning to NATO: "If you guys keep attacking Russia, you're part of the game. Game's over. I'm not playing anymore."

Moriarty believes the situation has progressed beyond the realm of hypothetical conflict: "There are guys a lot smarter than me saying we're not going into World War III. We are in World War III. And I tend to agree."

## The Energy Emergency Nobody Sees

Perhaps the most underappreciated crisis involves global energy supplies. The Strategic Petroleum Reserve has dropped dramatically from 416 million barrels to just 340 million barrels—a loss of approximately 75 million barrels in a very short timeframe.

"There's been almost no effect even though there's been a catastrophic reduction," Moriarty noted, expressing concern about the disconnect between supply fundamentals and prices.

The Cushing, Oklahoma storage hub—which holds approximately 1 million barrels of oil capacity and serves as the critical junction for U.S. pipeline networks—is at emergency levels. A closure of the Strait of Hormuz for even a week or two could cause oil prices to double or triple, yet markets appear oblivious to this risk.

"If you go back to 1973, at the start of the conflict after the Arab oil embargo, oil was $3 a barrel. Six months later, it was $12 a barrel," Moriarty recalled. "Cushing is at emergency levels. So literally a week or two closure of the strait could double or triple the price of oil, and nobody's prepared for it."

## The Military Capabilities Gap

Adding to the danger is the West's apparent lack of military preparedness. According to Moriarty, increasing production of critical weapons systems like Patriot missiles would take three to four years—time that may not be available if tensions continue escalating.

Meanwhile, Russia, China, and Iran possess advanced ballistic missile technology for which Western defenses remain largely ineffective.

"The scary thing is they have the kinetic power, and they can use that at any time," Moriarty warned. "But the scary thing is because we cannot respond in kinetic terms. We don't have the tanks, we don't have the aircraft, we don't have the missiles, we don't have the artillery."

## The Ukraine Stalemate

The Russia-Ukraine conflict, now entering its fifth year, represents another powder keg. Moriarty provided striking statistics about the ground reality: Russia is firing approximately ten times more artillery shells than Ukraine, resulting in Ukrainian casualties roughly ten times higher than Russian losses.

"I think the number of casualties including deaths is something above 2 million for Ukraine," Moriarty estimated. "Putin has made the mistake of trying to restrict the number of Russian deaths. While I appreciate that that's not the best way to win a war, he's winning the war at minimum casualty levels for his men."

The conflict has now lasted longer than World War II, with Moriarty suggesting that military-industrial complex interests may be prolonging the stalemate.

## Summary: Multiple Crises Converging

The convergence of several critical factors creates a landscape of unprecedented risk:

- **Markets** are experiencing simultaneous declines across nearly all asset classes
- **Geopolitical tensions** involving nuclear powers are escalating on multiple fronts
- **Energy supplies** sit at dangerously low levels with limited buffer capacity
- **Military capabilities** may be insufficient to respond to potential threats
- **Central bank policies** and AI-driven trading add additional layers of uncertainty

"We're in the early innings of the crash," Moriarty concluded. "Russia is threatening World War III. This war has lasted longer than World War II did."

For investors and observers alike, the message appears clear: the convergence of financial market stress with geopolitical instability creates a uniquely dangerous moment in global history—one that demands careful attention and preparation.
