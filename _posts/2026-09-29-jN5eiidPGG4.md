## The Spike in Treasury Yields: How High Can They Go?

Long-term Treasury yields have climbed to multi-decade highs, with benchmark rates placing heavy pressure on interest-rate-sensitive sectors such as utilities and real estate. Looking at the long-term technical charts for both the 10-year and 30-year yields, Vermeulen highlighted multi-year bullish flag formations.

Using Fibonacci extension analysis—measuring price momentum after consolidation pauses—Vermeulen pointed out that the 10-year yield exhibits technical projections toward the 6.5% mark, with an extreme upper extension near 8.0%.

While yields reaching those levels would represent a severe headwind for the broader economy and corporate borrowing costs, Vermeulen emphasized following the price action. Because the intermediate and long-term moving averages remain sloped upward, the prevailing technical trend for yields remains firmly higher until a distinct reversal pattern forms.

## Equities: Panic Selling Signals Short-Term Rebound

Despite broader market concerns and deteriorating market breadth across individual stock components, Vermeulen remains technically constructive on equities in the immediate term. 

Key technical observations include:

* **Panic Selling Indicators:** A spike in the custom panic selling indicator and elevated New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) volume ratios often mark short-term cycle lows, where retail capitulation leads to a 1-to-3 day turnaround.
* **Bullish Flag Patterns:** Both the NASDAQ composite futures and semiconductor sectors (such as the SMH ETF) maintain constructive bull flag setups.
* **Upside Targets:** The NASDAQ displays short-term momentum potential for a 3% to 5% rally, with a broader technical target pointing toward a potential 15% upside move.

Vermeulen noted that although the market is exhibiting characteristics of a late-stage (Stage 3) topping phase with notable sector rotation, strong megacap tech leaders continue to buoy index performance. Until a clear breakdown signal is triggered, momentum favors remaining aligned with the upward trend.

## The U.S. Dollar and Global Capital Flows

A rising yield environment continues to direct capital toward U.S. dollar-denominated assets. Technically, the U.S. Dollar Index (DXY) remains within an upward channel on monthly timeframes, with long-term upside projections extending toward prior cyclical highs around 120.

## Outlook for Gold and Precious Metals

In contrast to equities and the U.S. dollar, precious metals have entered what Vermeulen classifies as a Stage 4 downtrend. When an asset's price falls below its downward-sloping 150-day moving average, historical trends indicate sustained underperformance or protracted sideways action.

* **Macro Headwinds:** Rising real yields, a surging dollar, and the potential for margin-driven liquidations during equity market pullbacks create substantial headwinds for gold and silver.
* **Downside Price Targets:** Technical patterns point to a potential downside correction for gold toward the $3,100/oz level.
* **Long-Term Utility:** Vermeulen views precious metals primarily as a long-term insurance policy against systemic crisis rather than an active momentum vehicle during technical downtrends. A sharp pullback would serve as a major reset and buying opportunity for the next macro super-cycle.

## Bitcoin Shows Renewed Technical Strength

Unlike precious metals, Bitcoin has recently reversed its intermediate downtrend by breaking above its long-term moving averages and clearing prior swing highs. 

Following a standard Fibonacci pullback to the 0.618 retracement level, the technical structure indicates a potential 100% measured move. Vermeulen suggested that capital rotating away from precious metals and miners may be finding a temporary home in the digital asset space as momentum returns.
