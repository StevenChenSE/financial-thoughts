## Silver Mountain: A High-Grade Opportunity in Sonora

BRS Resources’ flagship international asset is the Silver Mountain project in the Sonora region of Mexico. The company holds over 1,000 hectares in this highly mining-friendly jurisdiction. Coulthard highlights the exceptional surface sampling results already obtained from the site:

*   **Gold:** Over 2 grams per tonne
*   **Silver:** 76 grams per tonne
*   **Copper:** 1.2%

The project is currently in the early drilling phase, utilizing a portable diamond drill rig capable of producing two to three holes per day at depths of 20 meters. The goal is to complete at least 30 holes to generate a maiden resource estimate. Coulthard notes that this lightweight approach minimizes environmental footprint—leaving only a small hole in the ground—and significantly reduces costs compared to traditional heavy machinery operations.

The geological team, led by a 45-year veteran Hall of Fame geologist, believes the property could hold between two and five million ounces of gold. While silver is present as a significant byproduct (estimated at around 40% of potential value), BRS positions itself primarily as a gold company with valuable copper and silver credits.
