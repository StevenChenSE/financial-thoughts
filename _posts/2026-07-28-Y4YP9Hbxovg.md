## Navigating the AI Boom, Precious Metals, and Digital Assets

In a candid interview, *Tucker Letter* editor EB Tucker shares his perspectives on the current market landscape, addressing the AI infrastructure boom, the performance of gold and silver, and the long-term potential of Bitcoin. Tucker emphasizes a proactive investment philosophy, urging investors to view constant change as an opportunity rather than a threat.

### The AI Infrastructure Race and Chip Volatility

Tucker addresses the recent sell-off in chip stocks, noting declines in Intel and SK Hynix, while highlighting the massive $250 billion backstop discussion between Nvidia and OpenAI for data centers in Pike County, Ohio. He argues that this is not a bubble burst but an evolution. "Data is the new gold," Tucker states, explaining that the demand for compute and scale is driving unprecedented innovation. He dismisses fears of an AI collapse, pointing out that Moore’s Law is still accelerating and that society will never return to pre-digital workflows. While acknowledging waste and cyclicality, he views the current tech surge as a historic opportunity for those who adapt.

### Gold, Silver, and the Leverage Factor

Contrary to popular belief, Tucker recently sold portions of his gold and silver holdings. He clarifies that he is not bearish on gold but believes prices had become "too hot." He attributes gold’s price movements primarily to leverage ratios in the futures market rather than interest rates or macroeconomic forecasts. Tucker advises investors to treat gold as a stable foundation for their portfolio rather than a vehicle for drastic returns, warning against over-allocation. He also criticizes mining stocks, arguing that many executives have failed to capitalize on gold’s recent highs and should be replaced if they cannot deliver shareholder value.

### Bitcoin: The Digital Buy

Tucker draws a sharp distinction between gold and Bitcoin. While he sees gold as a static store of value, he views Bitcoin as a critical component of a fully digitized future. He suggests that investors hold a small percentage of assets in Bitcoin, noting its market cap is significantly lower than gold’s, leaving room for growth. "The only coin I want to buy is Bitcoin," he asserts, dismissing other cryptocurrencies. He believes Bitcoin’s blockchain is an epic creation that will remain valuable as the world transitions to digital systems.

### Rejecting Collapse Narratives

A recurring theme in Tucker’s philosophy is the rejection of societal collapse narratives. He argues that hoarding gold for an apocalypse is flawed logic, as such scenarios would likely render physical assets useless or dangerous to hold. Instead, he advocates for engaging with the changing world, enjoying life, and making money through intelligent, human-driven investment decisions. He emphasizes the value of human-created content and experiences, urging investors to stay active, informed, and optimistic about the future.
