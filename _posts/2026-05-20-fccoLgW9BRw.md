## A Multipolar Power Shift You Can't Afford to Ignore

The recent China summit between President Trump and Xi Jinping wasn't merely a diplomatic photo opportunity—it was a carefully orchestrated signal to the rest of the world about the emerging power structure that will define the coming decades. In a wide-ranging discussion, macro analyst Simon Dixon unpacked how this meeting, combined with the Russia-China summit and a series of financial developments, reveals a coordinated transition toward a multipolar world order where the traditional Western-led system is being systematically renegotiated.

## The China Summit: More Than Meets the Eye

### Symbolic Displays of Power

The summit in Shanghai delivered unmistakable messages about shifting geopolitical dynamics:

- **Chair Height Discrepancy**: Xi Jinping sat in a higher chair while Trump occupied a lower one—a deliberate visual hierarchy
- **Arrival Protocol**: Xi did not personally greet Trump upon arrival, a departure from standard diplomatic protocol
- **Corporate Representation**: The real power structure of America was on display—executives from BlackRock, State Street, Vanguard, and major tech companies sat alongside Xi and the Chinese Communist Party leadership

This wasn't a negotiation session. According to Dixon, "The deals have been negotiated over an extended—even more than a decade—time as we're progressing to this managed transition." These public demonstrations simply signal to the world that the new order has been established and other nations should adjust their strategies accordingly.

### The DeepSeek Disruption

Perhaps the most significant development overshadowed by diplomatic theater was China's breakthrough with DeepSeek AI. This system achieves approximately 90% of the capability of America's leading AI at roughly one-tenth of the cost—and critically, it operates with zero dependency on American technology. DeepSeek is now fully integrated across:

- Hardware systems
- Software applications  
- Energy infrastructure
- The broader Huawei ecosystem

Combined with China's massive national investment fund backing Huawei and other tech giants, this represents a fundamental challenge to American AI supremacy. While the U.S. builds approximately 5,000 data centers to China's 500, the efficiency gap is closing rapidly.

### The Trade Deal Theater

The much-publicized agreements contained mostly recycled commitments:

- Purchasing American agricultural products—a return to pre-trade-war levels
- Buying American oil and gas—resumption of purchases suspended in early 2025
- Allowing Nvidia chip sales—a hollow gesture since China has deliberately pivoted toward self-sufficiency

The only substantive element, Dixon argues, was the strategic ambiguity surrounding the Strait of Hormuz and Iran's nuclear program—topics China chose not to publicly confirm or deny.

## The Strait of Hormuz: The Real Prize

### Why Everyone Wants It Closed

The strategic importance of the Strait of Hormuz cannot be overstated. Approximately 20-25% of global oil shipments transit this narrow waterway, making its status central to worldwide energy markets. The current closure benefits multiple parties:

- **Russia and Iran**: Higher oil prices and sanction relief
- **American LNG exporters**: Premium pricing for energy exports to Europe
- **Financial interests**: Higher commodity prices support bond valuations
- **Deal makers**: A closed strait creates urgency for bilateral agreements at elevated prices

The closure allows all major powers to renegotiate contracts at higher values and forces Europe into new LNG supply arrangements with America.

### The July Deadline and IPO Timing

NATO's recent statement about preparing to deploy to the Strait if it remains closed by July reveals a coordinated timeline. Dixon suggests this deadline aligns with some of the largest IPOs in history:

- **SpaceX**: Valued at approximately $2 trillion
- **OpenAI**: Valued at approximately $1 trillion

The strategic timing is clear: "If I'm Goldman Sachs, I don't want the strait open. I want to time the announcement of the opening of the strait with probably post IPO or with the IPO." This would create maximum valuation uplift before structural repricing occurs.

### The Unfolding Cascade

Foreign purchasing of U.S. bonds is increasingly routing through the Cayman Islands, indicating structural pressure. A dangerous sequence is developing:

1. Sustained Strait closure drives higher energy prices
2. Commodity prices up 33% year-to-date (CRB Index)
3. European and Asian economies face distress from energy costs
4. Distressed economies begin selling Treasury holdings
5. Yields spike further, making government debt refinancing unsustainable
6. Justification emerges for massive Fed bond purchases

The current environment combines the internet boom valuations, 2007 financial crisis yields, and 1973 energy crisis dynamics—creating what Dixon describes as "the perfect dotcom boom and bust narrative."

## The Multipolar Financial Infrastructure

### Preparing for Currency Diversification

The Financial Industrial Complex (FIC) has been preparing for a world where no single currency dominates:

**Key Financial Centers Being Developed:**
- United Arab Emirates (UAE)
- Hong Kong
- Saudi Arabia (opening markets)
- Iranian stock market (returning online)
- Venezuelan markets

Visa and Mastercard's presence at the China summit signals integration opportunities with China's SWIFT alternative (CIPS). The goal is hedging away from exposure to a single currency, capital market, and financial system.

### The Petro Dollar's Unraveling

The UAE's departure from OPEC represents the final structural blow to the petrodollar system. The timing coincided with UAE gaining access to Federal Reserve FX swap lines—the mechanism that protects the dollar peg without requiring oil sales into the system.

**What's Replacing the Petrodollar:**

- BRICS energy agreements (shelving the currency unit in favor of tokenized energy infrastructure)
- UAE as the center for crypto transactions
- Sanction evasion infrastructure running through UAE
- New FX swap lines and central bank digital currency bridges

This isn't accidental. The system is being deliberately rebuilt with multiple nodes and redundancies.

## The Russia-China Axis

### Putin's Beijing Visit

The subsequent Russia-China summit (with Putin meeting Xi Jinping in China) furthers the realignment:

**Key Agenda Items:**
- Bilateral relations expansion
- Power of Siberia 2 energy pipeline (accelerating)
- Trade agreements
- Regional security cooperation
- Dozens of agreements expected to be signed

Xi extended his hand to shake Putin's hand—unlike the protocol with Trump, where the foreign minister handled arrival greetings. This subtle difference signals the deeper relationship between Beijing and Moscow.

### The New Power Structure

Dixon emphasizes this isn't about hostility but coordination: "I still see this as way more coordinated than hostile. And the thought of nuclear war—that all of the targets and infrastructure rebuild that will come as a result of this is going to be funded by the BRICS corridors, the GCC corridors, the World Economic Forum elites, and obviously the most important node in the sovereign wealth fund is China."

Trump, in this framework, represents elite financial and technical power from both AI banks and the World Economic Forum—transitioning the world alongside Putin and Xi, not against them.

Dixon notes: "There is not enough energy in the world to power that. So they're building capacity to power it. And I believe that elites know that the next enemy is the domestic population."

## Iran's Bitcoin Financial System

A less-covered development involves Iran's construction of a parallel financial infrastructure:

**Bypassing Traditional Systems:**
- Lloyd's of London (through alternative insurance contracts)
- SWIFT (using Bitcoin)
- Maritime protection (via multi-signature Bitcoin wallets)

After Western asset seizures, Iran pivoted to cryptocurrency. Crucially, when Tether froze stablecoins and a foundation called Arbitrum froze altcoins, they could not touch Bitcoin. Iran has been mining Bitcoin with nuclear energy to rebuild an internal legal financial system.

This represents a template for sanctioned nations to maintain financial functionality outside Western control systems.

This shift represents the underlying monetary architecture supporting the new multipolar order.
