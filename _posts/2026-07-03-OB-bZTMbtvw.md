## Anonymous Investors Pour Tens of Millions Into Gold Call Options as Market Bottom Appears to Be In

The precious metals market is witnessing one of the most significant insider positioning events in recent memory. According to market analyst Ed Steer, anonymous investors have accumulated over 30,000 gold call options contracts for December with strike prices at $10,000, $15,000, and $20,000—an unprecedented bet that gold will reach five figures before the year concludes.

Speaking on the Capital Cosm podcast, Steer revealed that this massive positioning began in February when approximately 20,000 contracts were visible. "Now it's up to over 30,000," he stated. "So there's some serious money being bet that we're going to see a fantastically higher gold price before the end of the year."

## The Bottom Is In: Commercial Traders Reach Their Limit

Despite gold and silver experiencing a prolonged downturn since January highs, Steer maintains that the market has already hit its nadir. His analysis centers on the behavior of commercial traders—the major players who have been aggressively suppressing prices by covering short positions.

"The commercial traders have been pounding away trying to cover as many shorts as they can," Steer explained. "They've reached the bottom now. They haven't been able to improve their position for the last month or so."

The key dynamic at play involves the law of diminishing returns. Once long positions in the managed money and reportable trader categories reach a point where participants refuse to sell or open new shorts—particularly when prices fall significantly below the 200-day moving average—commercial traders lose their ability to push prices lower, regardless of their efforts.

"Unless the longs are prepared to sell or go short, the price can't go any lower," Steer declared. "I don't care how hard they try."

## The Hidden Accumulation Story: Silver Stocks Defying Gravity

Perhaps the most compelling evidence of institutional accumulation lies not in the metals themselves, but in their equities. Year-to-date performance through June reveals a striking divergence:

- Gold price: down 7.35%
- Gold stocks (HUI Index): down 8.15%
- Silver price: down 18%
- Silver stocks: down only 2.39%

"The silver stocks are only down 2.39%," Steer emphasized. "So there's a massive difference between how the silver stocks are performing versus the gold stocks versus their respective metal prices."

This discrepancy is mathematically impossible without substantial buying pressure. For silver stocks to perform relatively well while silver itself drops 18% requires massive accumulation—somebody is positioning for future appreciation while the retail market remains focused on the short-term downturn.

## Markets Under Management: No Price Discovery Allowed

Steer's assessment of current market conditions is stark: "Whatever you see on your screen right now when you're looking at any financial product is a total hallucination."

The veteran analyst argues that all markets are heavily managed—from bonds and currencies to equities and commodities. The Federal Reserve's intervention in the 10-year Treasury yield, which reached approximately 4.5% before being pushed back, exemplifies this manipulation.

"We are living in the biggest financial and paper bubble the world has ever known," Steer stated. "It far exceeds 1929, the tulip mania, and the subprime bubble. We have never been in anything like this before."

Since Nixon ended the gold window in 1971, the global monetary system has operated in what Steer calls a "paper fantasy world." The dollar and other currencies display parabolic charts heading toward worthless, while central banks print money at unprecedented rates.

## Structural Deficits Across All Precious Metals

Beyond price manipulation, fundamental supply-demand imbalances are building. Gold exploration has failed to discover sufficient reserves to replace annual production of approximately 125 million ounces. "There hasn't been a monster deposit of gold found in the last 10 or 15 years," Steer noted. "Even if they find it, it's going to take them another 10 years to bring it online."

Central banks have absorbed over 1,000 tons of gold annually, creating structural deficits that will eventually overwhelm paper market suppression. The same pattern exists in platinum, copper, and other critical commodities.

"When the physical market runs out—and that's when it will make a difference—the sky's the the limit as far as I'm concerned," Steer predicted. "It's just a matter of when it's going to happen."

## Investment Strategy: Buying When Blood Runs in the Streets

For investors navigating what Steer describes as a rigged market, his advice is straightforward: accumulate at bottoms and hold for the inevitable rally.

"I've been fully invested in precious metals for 20 years, and of course, after 20 years, you're obviously up quite a bit," he shared, noting recent personal purchases in mining stocks as prices declined.

His trading framework focuses on "wash, rinse, and spin cycles"—periods when commercial traders allow prices to rise, cover shorts, then suppress prices again to repeat the process. These cyclical rallies provide accumulation opportunities for patient investors.

"You buy when blood is running in the streets," Steer advised. "Buy where others are afraid to buy and sell when others won't sell anything."

## The Great Reset: When Paper Markets Blow Up

The interview's most provocative segment addressed the eventual resolution of this manipulated system. Steer suggested that China's rumored holding of 20,000+ tons of undeclared gold could fundamentally reshape markets upon disclosure.

But the deeper issue involves the viability of the entire paper currency system, which Steer believes has reached its terminus.

"How they're going to handle it, I don't know," Steer admitted. "But I can tell you for sure that it's going to result in a fantastically higher gold price than we have today."

The current $20,000 call options represent serious institutional conviction that a revaluation event—perhaps comparable to Nixon's 1971 announcement—will occur before year end.

"That will be it," Steer concluded. "People talk about the great reset. That will be it."
