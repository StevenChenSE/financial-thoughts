## The Fed’s "Watchful Waiting" Stuns Markets
The recent Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting concluded with rates held unchanged, but it was the subsequent press conference by Chair John Walsh that dominated market sentiment. He emphasized that there is no "soft" 2% inflation target, clarifying that the Fed remains resolute on price stability but is not committed to immediate rate hikes to achieve it.

Walsh stated that normal nominal and real yields are materially higher and that markets should continue to respond to incoming data rather than relying on forward guidance. This approach, effectively giving markets a "green light" to price in their own expectations, led to a significant repricing.
