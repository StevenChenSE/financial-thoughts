## Technical Analysis of Gold's Wedge Pattern and Silver's Critical Support Zones

Gareth Soloway, Chief Market Strategist at Verified Investing.com, provides a comprehensive technical analysis of the precious metals market, focusing on the immediate price action in gold and silver. Recent market movements have been influenced by macroeconomic data, including a weaker jobs report that reduced the probability of interest rate hikes. Additionally, a pullback in the US Dollar Index (DXY) and the 10-year yield has provided supportive tailwinds for metals prices.

### Gold's Wedge Pattern and Imminent Breakout

The primary focus of the gold analysis is a descending wedge pattern, a technical formation characterized by two converging trend lines that compress price action over time. The upper resistance line connects the highs established in March and May, while the lower support line has been tested repeatedly. Soloway notes that the lower trend line has been hit five times, which technically increases the probability of a decisive breakout or breakdown beyond a standard 50/50 likelihood.

Gold is approaching the "head" of the wedge, indicating that a major price decision is due by early to mid-August. Two distinct scenarios are on the table:

*   **Bullish Breakout:** A confirmed break above the upper resistance, currently hovering around the $4,250 level, would signal the start of a significant bull move. This could propel gold back toward all-time highs and potentially initiate a long-term trend toward $10,000 per ounce within the next two to three years.
*   **Bearish Breakdown:** Conversely, a rejection at resistance and a breakdown below the wedge could trigger a flush down to the $3,500 to $3,600 range. Soloway maintains a short-term bias toward this downside target, viewing the dip below $4,000 as an accumulation zone for long-term investors.

### Silver's Technical Outlook and Key Levels

Silver currently appears weaker relative to gold, with price action struggling to reclaim critical levels. A trend line that previously served as support at $64 per ounce has been broken and confirmed as resistance. For silver to demonstrate relative strength, it must first reconfirm above the $64 level.

To flip the chart structure from bearish to bullish, silver needs to break above a major resistance level at $72. Failure to hold current support could lead to a decline toward $54, with potential washout levels at $50 and $46. These lower targets align with historical price structures from the 1980 and 2011 highs, offering long-term value despite short-term volatility.

### Market Psychology and Macro Factors

Soloway emphasizes that short-term price movements are often driven by human emotion and psychology rather than fundamental valuations. Market flushouts serve to clear weak hands and maximize bearish sentiment, creating optimal entry points for seasoned investors. While inflation-adjusted metrics suggest higher valuations for silver, immediate price action remains subject to emotional trading dynamics.

The interplay between the dollar, bond yields, and employment data continues to shape the metals market. As gold nears the apex of its wedge pattern, traders are advised to monitor the $4,250 level for confirmation of the next major trend. Meanwhile, silver requires a decisive break above $72 to validate a new bull run, while downside risks remain active toward the $50 mark.

### Summary

Gold is approaching a critical technical juncture within a descending wedge pattern, with a breakout above $4,250 or a breakdown toward the $3,500 to $3,600 range expected by August. Silver remains under pressure, needing to reclaim $64 and break $72 to confirm bullish momentum, while downside targets include $50 and $46. Despite short-term volatility, the long-term outlook remains bullish, with significant upside potential projected for both metals over the coming years.
