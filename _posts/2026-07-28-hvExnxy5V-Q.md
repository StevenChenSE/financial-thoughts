## Wyckoff Accumulation: A Potential Reversal Pattern

While Elliott Wave points downward, the Wyckoff accumulation pattern presents a scenario where Bitcoin might form only one more marginally lower low before rallying. This pattern, identified by trader Richard Wyckoff in the early 1900s, describes how "big money" accumulates positions quietly during periods of low interest and high boredom.

### The Five Phases of Wyckoff Accumulation

1.  **Phase A (Preliminary Support & Panic):** The initial downtrend dies, followed by a panic phase where volume explodes downward, and smart money begins buying. The subsequent automatic rally (AR) occurs because sellers are exhausted.
2.  **Phase B (Building the Position):** This is the "boring" phase where price chops sideways for months. Sentiment is low, news is negative, and big players build positions piece by piece. The July low marks a secondary test (ST) within this phase.
3.  **Phase C (The Spring):** This is the critical phase. Price breaks below support (below February/July lows) to trigger stop losses and force capitulation from the last holders. Crucially, the price must reclaim the zone almost immediately on low volume, signaling that supply is exhausted.
4.  **Phase D (Sign of Strength):** Higher lows and higher highs form, often accompanied by a breakout through resistance on real volume. A pullback here offers the last entry opportunity.
5.  **Phase E (Markup):** The range is broken, and the true uptrend begins.
