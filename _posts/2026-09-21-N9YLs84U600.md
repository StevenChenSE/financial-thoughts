*   **Scrap Sales:** Between 1998 and 2002, approximately 500 tankers were sold for scrap.

*   **Pricing Power:** Used tankers are now trading at a premium to new ones, reflecting the scarcity of capacity.

Nikolovski suggests that this bottleneck could lead to a secular bull market in tanker stocks, similar to the 2002–2009 period. He also notes that chemical stocks, which historically move with oil due to pricing power, may begin to correlate again as energy prices remain elevated.
