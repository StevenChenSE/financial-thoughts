## Introduction: The Capitulation Moment in Precious Metals

On October 22, 2026, macro strategist Tavi Costa joined host Danny on *Capital Calls* to dissect a critical inflection point in the financial markets. While global equities faced significant headwinds—with the NASDAQ down substantially and tech giants like Meta seeing sharp declines due to free cash flow concerns—Costa identified two "massive signals" that suggest the precious metals sector, particularly mining equities, may be approaching a historic buying opportunity.

Despite gold clinging to the $4,000 level and silver hovering around $54, Costa argues that the current volatility is not the end of the bull market, but rather a "capitulation-type move" driven by extreme oversold conditions in both metal prices and their primary macro drivers: interest rates and the US dollar.

## The Dollar’s Failure to Rally: A Critical Clue

The most significant signal Costa highlights is the behavior of the US Dollar Index (DXY) during market liquidations. Typically, during deflationary shocks or major stock sell-offs, the dollar acts as a safe haven, rallying against other currencies. However, on this day, with the NASDAQ down approximately 3% and oil rising, the dollar failed to catch a bid, instead dropping by 17 basis points.

Costa describes this divergence as "without a doubt the most important clue of today's market reaction." He argues that:

1.  **Unsustainable Strength:** The recent rally in the dollar was always unsustainable given extreme long positioning and an overweight view on US assets.
2.  **Policy Mandate:** The Treasury Secretary clearly does not want a strong dollar, making sustained strength unlikely.
3.  **Exhaustion:** The lack of safe-haven flows into the USD suggests the currency has peaked.

When good news or risk-off events do not translate into price action for an asset it traditionally benefits from, it often signals that further downside is imminent. Costa predicts the dollar will weaken substantially relative to other fiat currencies and hard assets in the coming months.

## Treasury Yields Rolling Over: The Second Signal

The second major signal involves the US Treasury market. While financial media had previously screamed about a bond market implosion at peak yields, Costa took a contrarian approach by acquiring call options on Treasuries during the decline. He notes that yields are finally rolling over, and the Treasury market is stabilizing.

For precious metals, falling real yields and stabilizing bond prices are fundamental tailwinds. Costa emphasizes that you cannot look at one driver in isolation; it is a combination of factors that creates opportunity. When both the dollar weakens and Treasury yields stabilize or fall, these two factors align decisively in favor of the mining space.

## Mining Equities: A Case for Aggressive Deployment

Costa views the current state of gold and silver miners as an extreme oversold scenario comparable to the lows seen during the 2008 Global Financial Crisis. With gold prices down roughly 30% from peak levels, he argues that panic selling is irrational at these valuations.

### Fundamental Improvements in Miners
Unlike three years ago, today’s mining companies offer:
*   **Better Margins:** Improved operational efficiency and cost management.
*   **Strong Balance Sheets:** Outstanding cash positions and reduced debt.
*   **Attractive Valuations:** Historically low price-to-earnings multiples relative to asset value.

### Technicals and Sentiment
Costa points out that the breadth of the mining market is weak, with most companies trading below their 200-day moving averages—a sign of capitulation. He advises investors to study historical bottoms (such as those in 2008 or during recent geopolitical tensions), where charts looked "awful" and violated every technical support line just before major rebounds began.

He recommends a shift from conservative cash holdings (25-30%) to more aggressive deployment (<10% cash) for those with conviction, utilizing dollar-cost averaging rather than trying to time the exact bottom.

## The Silver Squeeze Thesis

A specific highlight of Costa’s analysis is the outlook for silver stocks. He argues that silver equities are currently overweighted in his portfolio due to a unique confluence of factors:
*   **Valuation:** Cheap entry points.
*   **Margins:** Strong profitability at current metal prices.

Costa predicts that as silver companies announce excess cash for buybacks, the scarcity of available shares could drive a violent repricing. He emphasizes that this thesis currently looks "dumb" to most investors, which is precisely why it is an attractive asymmetric bet.
