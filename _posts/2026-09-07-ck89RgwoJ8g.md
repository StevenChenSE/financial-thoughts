## Critical Minerals: The New Frontier

Beyond gold and silver, Feneck places significant weight on critical minerals, a sector he has overweighted since 2023. He argues that China has "eaten lunch" for U.S. investors in this space, controlling 65% to 95% of production in key categories like tungsten, rare earths, and tantalum. This dominance has led to severe supply shortages, with countries like Japan being cut off from supplies, creating a perfect storm for U.S. and allied producers.

Feneck highlights three specific critical mineral plays:

1.  **Guardian Metals (GMTL/GMTLF):** A U.S.-based tungsten play that Feneck calls his top holding in the sector. The company has two projects and recently released a Pre-Feasibility Study (PFS) showing a first-year EBITDA of $348 million and a payback period of 0.9 years. With tungsten prices surging from $920 to over $2,800 per unit, Feneck sees significant upside for Guardian, which he believes is undervalued despite its strong metrics.
2.  **Western Star Resources (WSR):** A micro-cap company with three projects in the U.S., including the highest-grade tungsten samples he has seen (3-4% tungsten in Nevada, compared to a global average of 0.3%). Feneck notes that the company is actively drilling and has a small market cap, making it a high-risk, high-reward play.
3.  **First Tantalum (FSTTF):** A company focused on tantalum, a metal 65% produced in China. Feneck highlights the company’s "PyroDelta" division, which produces drone-based heating and cooling technology. With the drone market estimated at $150,000 to $3 million per unit, Feneck sees a potential revenue catalyst that could drive the stock from its current levels back to previous highs.
