## The Silver Capitulation Event

Vermeulen notes that silver’s recent action unfolded largely as planned. After hitting a major high in March, technical charts pointed to a downside target of $39. On November 25th, silver hit this level, flushing down to approximately $38 before rallying back above $40.

"This is a huge pivotal day... for precious metals," Vermeulen stated. He identified the move as a "washout low" or "shakeout," characterized by an exhaustion gap on the 30-minute chart. The daily chart painted a bullish candle, signaling that sentiment had reached an extreme oversold condition.

Key technical factors contributing to the rally included:
*   **Capitulation:** Panic selling from equities bled into metals, causing investors to give up at the lows.
*   **Short Covering:** With the Thanksgiving weekend approaching, traders holding short positions covered their bets to avoid weekend risk.
*   **Accumulation:** Buyers stepped in aggressively at the $39 technical support level.

## Defensive Strategy: Sitting on Hands

Given the uncertainty, Vermeulen’s primary strategy is capital preservation. He advocates for sitting in cash during periods of high volatility, specifically using ETFs like **BOXX** (an exchange-traded fund that holds short-term US Treasuries) to earn interest while waiting for clear buy signals.

"The big view is to sit on your hands during this volatility because it's actually really dangerous," Vermeulen warned. He emphasized that trying to time the exact bottom in a falling knife scenario often leads to significant losses. His approach involves:
1.  Locking in profits when trends change.
2.  Avoiding emotional trading based on news headlines.
3.  Waiting for confirmed bullish chart patterns before re-entering markets.
