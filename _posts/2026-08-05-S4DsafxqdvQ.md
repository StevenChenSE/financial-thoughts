## A fundamental shift is occurring beneath the surface of precious metals markets

On the surface, the gold and silver markets appear stagnant, prompting many investors to wait on the sidelines. However, Daniel Wilhelmi of *Hot Trades of the Day* argues that a critical, overlooked development in the equity markets is flashing a major bullish signal for silver. While technical charts of spot silver show consolidation, a breakout in the solar energy sector suggests a coming surge in industrial demand that could propel silver prices higher.

## Silver Price Technical Analysis and Dependence on Gold

At first glance, silver spot prices show little activity. Wilhelmi notes that while silver is making higher lows and has successfully broken a downtrend channel, it remains far from a confirmed bull run. Crucially, silver’s performance is heavily tethered to gold.

Gold recently formed a triple bottom on the daily chart, with the third bottom higher than the second. This structure indicates a slight technical improvement and a confirmed break of a downtrend that had persisted since early March. However, Wilhelmi emphasizes that gold lacks buying momentum, as evidenced by its Relative Strength Index (RSI), and has yet to clear key resistance levels at the 50-day moving average and $4,215. Until gold stabilizes and gains upward traction, silver is unlikely to break out independently.

## The Solar Sector: Silver’s Biggest Industrial Demand Driver

The overlooked bullish catalyst lies in the solar energy sector, which represents the number one industrial use case for silver. Wilhelmi points to the TAN ETF, a major solar stock exchange-traded fund, which recently broke a significant downtrend channel that had been in place for over two months.

Although the ETF remains below its 50-day and 200-day moving averages—explaining why mainstream analysts haven’t caught wind of this yet—the technical break is a leading indicator. Wilhelmi highlights First Solar (FSLR), a stock he previously recommended, which has surged approximately 15% in just 10 days following a similar breakout. This individual stock strength suggests broader positive sentiment returning to the renewable energy space.

## Geopolitical Shifts Driving the Solar Boom

The resurgence of the solar sector is not merely a technical phenomenon but is driven by fundamental geopolitical realities. Wilhelmi explains that ongoing conflicts involving Iran and instability in the Strait of Hormuz have introduced a risk premium to oil prices. This uncertainty is prompting countries to rethink their energy dependencies.

With shifting power dynamics in the Middle East and changing US foreign policy expectations heading into 2026, nations are increasingly prioritizing energy independence. This shift is expected to drive a "second boom" in solar, wind, and other renewable energies as countries build out strategic reserves and reduce reliance on Middle Eastern oil. For silver investors, this impending surge in solar manufacturing capacity serves as a powerful fundamental demand driver.

## Solar Stocks: V-Shaped Recoveries and Market Narratives

Investors accustomed to traditional chart patterns may be hesitant to enter the solar sector, as the TAN ETF does not currently display a classic double-bottom or W-formation. However, Wilhelmi notes that solar stocks historically behave differently, characterized by high short interest and rapid V-shaped recoveries following sharp sell-offs.

Historical data from 2023 and 2024 shows that solar stocks often rally explosively once selling pressure subsides and a downtrend breaks. Wilhelmi advises watching for a shift in market narrative. In financial markets, sentiment regarding facts often matters more than the facts themselves. As the narrative around solar and energy independence turns positive, it will inevitably spill over into the silver market.

## Investment Strategy: Avoiding the Binary Trap

Given these signals, Wilhelmi recommends that silver investors avoid a binary strategy of holding 100% cash while waiting for a final panic sell-off. Silver charts currently show a Wyckoff accumulation phase, but there is a genuine possibility that the anticipated "spring" or liquidity grab (a final downward undercut) may not materialize.

To prevent being left behind as mining stocks and silver prices begin to grind higher, Wilhelmi suggests putting a portion of capital to work now—whether in silver, gold, or miners. This approach honors the probabilities shown by the chart: while a drop is still possible, the constructive price action and solar sector signals indicate that the bottom may already be in. By maintaining a flexible mindset and partial exposure, investors can position themselves to profit regardless of whether the market dips further or rallies immediately.
