## Introduction
Alex teams up with Dan from Micro Center to engineer the smallest possible AI workstation. The goal is to create a powerhouse that rivals the size of a Mac Studio and fits comfortably in a backpack. While waiting for the Velka 3 case to arrive, the team begins component selection and fitment testing in a larger Silverstone ML09 case before moving to the final ultra-compact build.

## Component Selection
*   **GPU:** The star of the build is the NVIDIA RTX 4000 SFF Ada Generation. This card offers 24GB of VRAM, the smallest footprint available for that memory capacity. It draws only 70W from the PCIe bus, eliminating the need for external power connectors, and features a low-profile bracket option essential for tight spaces.
*   **CPU and Motherboard:** To maximize efficiency and cooling headroom, the team selects the AMD Ryzen 5 9600X over the hotter Ryzen 9 9900X. The motherboard is the MSI B850I, a budget-friendly ITX board chosen over a pricier ASUS alternative.
*   **Memory and Storage:** Budget constraints lead to a 32GB DDR5 6400 CL32 kit from Crucial. The team notes that system RAM should roughly match VRAM to accommodate OS overhead while loading models.
*   **Power and Cooling:** A Flex Guru 650W FSP power supply is required for the Velka 3 form factor. Cooling is handled by a compact Thermalright cooler, with Noctua fans providing airflow.

## The Build Challenge
Installing components into the Velka 3 proves difficult. The team encounters issues with a PCIe 4 riser cable, eventually switching to a PCIe 5 riser to ensure compatibility. The GPU initially fails to clear the case with its bracket attached, requiring the bracket to be removed entirely. The build process involves multiple disassemblies and reassemblies, highlighting the tight tolerances of SFF builds.

## Performance Benchmarks
*   **Mac Mini Comparison:** Running Qwen 3 4B, the RTX 4000 SFF workstation achieves 103 tokens per second, significantly outperforming the Mac Mini's 30 tokens per second.
*   **Mac Studio M3 Ultra Comparison:** When tested against the M3 Ultra with a Gemma model, distinct strengths emerge:
    *   *Decode Speed:* The M3 Ultra leads with 45.4 tokens per second compared to the RTX 4000 SFF's 39.6 tokens per second, attributed to the Mac's superior memory bandwidth of 819GB/s versus the GPU's 672GB/s.
    *   *Prefill Speed:* The RTX 4000 SFF dominates prompt processing, delivering 1505 tokens per second against the Mac's 650 tokens per second, showcasing the power of CUDA cores for prefill workloads.

## Conclusion
The custom Velka 3 build succeeds in creating a highly portable AI workstation that fits in a backpack. While the M3 Ultra offers faster token generation due to massive memory bandwidth, the RTX 4000 SFF provides superior prompt processing speeds and dedicated VRAM advantages. The build demonstrates that compact AI workstations can deliver professional performance, with the choice depending on whether prefill speed or decode throughput is the priority.
