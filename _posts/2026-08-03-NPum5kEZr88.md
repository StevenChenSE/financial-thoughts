## A Contrarian View on the Precious Metals Sector

**By Danny, Capital Calls**

In a recent episode of *Capital Calls*, host Danny sat down with Brandon Bonifacio, CEO of NevGold (TSXV: NAU / OTC: NAUFF), to discuss the current state of the gold mining industry, the strategic importance of critical minerals, and the investment opportunities arising from recent market volatility. With gold trading above $4,000 per ounce and geopolitical tensions influencing macroeconomic indicators, Bonifacio shares his perspective on why the current pullback presents a prime entry point for investors.

### The Macro Environment: Headlines vs. Fundamentals

The current gold market is heavily influenced by geopolitical narratives, particularly kinetic action in the Middle East. This tension drives oil prices higher, which in turn fuels inflation expectations and pushes the US 10-year yield up. While these factors create headwinds for gold in the short term, Bonifacio argues that the fundamental backdrop remains robust.

"Gold is still $4,000 plus US," Bonifacio notes. "For gold exploring companies or gold miners, that is a very healthy position to be in." He emphasizes that many projects are generating significant free cash flow at these price levels. Despite daily oscillations, he predicts that as the summer slowdown concludes and markets stabilize in the fall, inflows will return to the sector, driving significant gains in the back half of 2026.

### NevGold: A Gold-Antimony Dual Thesis

NevGold has distinguished itself through a unique dual-commodity strategy. Initially focused on gold, the company made a significant discovery of antimony alongside its gold resources in Nevada and Idaho. This discovery has transformed NevGold into a holder of the largest domestic antimony resource in the United States.

Antimony is a critical mineral used in over 300 military weapons, devices, and every bullet fired globally. Given its strategic importance to national security, it has attracted attention from the US Department of War and the White House. Bonifacio highlights that while competitors like Perpetual Resources have received billions in government funding for antimony projects, NevGold’s resource is now larger, positioning the company well for potential government support and strategic partnerships.

"We are a gold-antimony company," Bonifacio states. "Both are very important value layers for us." The company’s projects are characterized by simple, near-surface oxide heap leach operations, which require low capital to build and mine, providing downside protection regardless of commodity price fluctuations.

### Value on the Lassonde Curve

Bonifacio identifies the exploration and development stage of the Lassonde Curve as the area of best value currently. While large-cap producers have priced in their cash flow multiples, exploration and development companies like NevGold are trading at sub-$50 per ounce of gold in the ground.

"We’ve got 4 million ounces between our two projects in Nevada and Idaho," Bonifacio explains. "Most projects now are getting acquired or invested into at $300 to $500 per ounce." This disparity suggests a 5x to 10x potential upside based on gold valuation alone, excluding the value of the antimony resource.

### Jurisdiction and Operational Efficiency

In an era of increasing nationalism and de-globalization, jurisdiction is paramount. NevGold’s flagship projects are located in Nevada and Idaho, jurisdictions Bonifacio describes as having straightforward permitting regimes and strong infrastructure. Unlike challenging international jurisdictions, these US-based projects avoid political surprises and align with the US government’s focus on internalizing critical mineral supply chains.

Furthermore, the simplicity of NevGold’s operations mitigates the impact of rising operating costs, such as fuel and labor. While large-scale miners face margin erosion from high all-in sustaining costs, NevGold’s smaller, efficient operations remain in the lowest cost decile, preserving margins even in inflationary environments.

### Financial Strength and Future Outlook

NevGold closed a well-timed financing in early June 2026, raising $42 million CAD, leaving the company with approximately $50 million in treasury. This capital position allows NevGold to advance its assets without the overhang of near-term financing needs. The company is focused on drilling, engineering studies, and permitting, with a path to potential gold and antimony production within the next 18 months.

Bonifacio encourages investors to take a contrarian approach. "When others are selling, if you have conviction over the short, medium, and long term, be a buyer," he advises. He views the recent 50% retracement in many gold stocks as a perfect setup for those who missed the previous run. With strong balance sheets, strategic assets, and a clear development path, NevGold is poised for growth as market momentum shifts back to the upside in the coming months.

Brandon Bonifacio, CEO of NevGold, highlights the strategic value of combining gold mining with critical mineral production, specifically antimony. With a strong balance sheet, low-cost operations in safe jurisdictions, and a significant resource base, NevGold represents a compelling opportunity for investors looking to capitalize on the next leg of the precious metals bull market.
