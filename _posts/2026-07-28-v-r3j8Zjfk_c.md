Geopolitical instability is adding a "war premium" to the market. With dollar dominance rising, investors may prefer to wait for a stronger dollar to buy more Bitcoin later, rather than buying at current highs. This dynamic suggests that Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies may face headwinds until the dollar weakens.
