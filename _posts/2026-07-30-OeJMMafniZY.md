## A New Era for Gold Mining Investments

Gold stocks are currently experiencing a significant surge, presenting what analysts describe as one of the best setups in years. This momentum is being driven by major geopolitical shifts, specifically recent changes in China’s financial markets. Filip Tortevski and senior analyst Pedro Banales from Wealth Within highlight three key ASX-listed companies poised for growth: Capricorn Metals, Evolution Mining, and Westgold Resources.

## The Impact of China Ending Paper Gold Trading

A pivotal event triggering this market shift is China’s decision to halt retail paper gold trading products linked to the Shanghai Gold Exchange. This move forced retail investors to either close positions, cash out, or take physical delivery. While the official stance cites investor protection against volatility, analysts suggest a deeper motive: shifting market focus from speculative paper pricing to natural price discovery based on physical demand.

Pedro Banales notes that while retail investors are not the dominant market force, this shift underscores China’s broader strategy regarding currency dominance. As speculative futures markets detach from physical reality, the push toward physical gold trading could support higher gold prices, benefiting mining companies directly.

## Capricorn Metals Ltd (ASX: CMM)

Capricorn Metals has announced a 33% increase in its gold reserves, now totaling over 5.2 million ounces. This expansion extends mine life and positions the company to produce over 400,000 ounces annually in the coming years. Notably, these reserve estimates were calculated using conservative gold prices of AUD $2,200 to $2,600 per ounce, highlighting substantial upside potential if prices remain elevated.

Technically, the stock has shown resilience, holding higher lows since May 2024. Analysts identify a key resistance level around AUD $14.20, which has been tested multiple times. A break above this level could propel the stock toward its all-time high of AUD $16.40 and potentially into the AUD $17–$18 range. However, investors should monitor support levels; a drop below AUD $11.20 or AUD $10.80 could signal a deeper correction toward AUD $9. Volume is currently compressing, suggesting a buildup for the next move.

## Evolution Mining Ltd (ASX: EVN)

Evolution Mining continues to expand its pipeline with a AUD $213 million acquisition of Carnaby Resources, securing the high-grade Greater Duchess project in Queensland. This deal aims to reduce development costs and unlock operational synergies with Evolution’s existing Ernest Henry operations.

Since its 2022 low, Evolution Mining has rallied significantly, gaining between 800% and 900%. However, the stock is currently undergoing a pullback, with five consecutive months of downward movement on the monthly chart. Bearish signals include wide opening and closing ranges that are now compressing, indicating seller exhaustion. The critical support level is around AUD $10.20. A hold above this line, accompanied by a break in momentum and increased volume, could trigger a sharp upward trend similar to previous rallies.

## Westgold Resources Ltd (ASX: WGX)

Westgold Resources is highlighted as a particularly mature and promising pick. The stock has broken through momentum barriers and is showing signs of reversal. A key technical indicator is the stock’s inability to push prices lower over the past five weeks, despite selling pressure. The June low of AUD $4.24 serves as a crucial support marker.

Westgold has pulled back to fill a previous gap, finding buying interest at this level. Closing above this gap would consolidate short-to-medium-term support, potentially opening a path back toward AUD $8. The stock closely tracks the price of gold, meaning continued strength in gold prices will likely drive Westgold higher. Recent data shows a 6% gap up, with volume increasing as the price stabilizes, ticking many boxes for a potential breakout above the AUD $5 level.

## Summary

The convergence of China’s shift toward physical gold trading and strong fundamental news from ASX miners creates a compelling investment case. Capricorn Metals offers reserve growth and upside potential, Evolution Mining provides operational synergies after a consolidation phase, and Westgold Resources shows strong technical reversal signals. Investors are advised to monitor key support and resistance levels, as well as overall gold price trends, to time entries effectively.
