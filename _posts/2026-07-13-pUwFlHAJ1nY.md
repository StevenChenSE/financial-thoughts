## July 9, 2026 Update: Geopolitical Tensions and Economic Vulnerabilities

On July 9, 2026, Danny of Capital Cosm hosted Bob Moriarty, publisher of 321gold.com, for a comprehensive analysis of the escalating global crisis. The discussion highlighted severe risks across energy markets, geopolitical conflicts, US military capabilities, and financial stability. Moriarty warned of an impending diesel shortage, aggressive market manipulation, and significant leadership concerns within the US administration, painting a picture of a fragile global system on the brink of chaos.

## The Imminent Diesel and Oil Crisis

Moriarty outlined a critical shortage developing in the oil market, noting that 1.3 to 1.5 million barrels of oil have been removed from the market. While this oil was produced, it has never been refined, creating a bottleneck that threatens global supply. Strategic reserves are depleted; the US Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) is at minimum levels, and Cushing, Oklahoma, which once stored a billion barrels, is now empty.

The US economy relies heavily on diesel for transportation, and Moriarty predicts a crisis will become obvious within weeks as fuel runs out. Despite the physical shortage, WTI crude oil prices remain near pre-war levels, trading between $66 and $76 per barrel compared to $67 before the conflict. Moriarty attributes this discrepancy to aggressive manipulation. He explained that while "naked short selling" is a myth in a zero-sum market where every contract has a buyer and seller, the administration could be manipulating prices by selling contracts and forcing a cover, which could trigger a massive price spike.

## Geopolitical Flashpoints and the Strait of Hormuz

The conversation addressed the ongoing war involving Israel, the United States, and Iran. Moriarty argued that Israel initiated the conflict to destroy Iran, dismissing the nuclear program as a "red herring." He suggested President Trump was dragged into the war by Benjamin Netanyahu to prevent the release of the Epstein files.

A key focus was the Strait of Hormuz. Moriarty contended that Iran effectively closed the strait not just through military action, but by leveraging insurance companies. Without insurance, cargo owners will not route ships through the region. He also highlighted Iran's innovative use of drones as defensive decoys. By firing cheap drones as deliberate targets, Iran forces adversaries to expend expensive Patriot missiles, creating an economic attrition war where Iran holds the advantage. Reports of a US destroyer being hit by a drone were cited as a potential game-changer that could escalate the conflict dramatically.

## US Military Weakness and Technological Failures

He criticized the Patriot and Iron Dome systems as ineffective and too expensive against drone swarms. Furthermore, he argued that US aircraft carriers are obsolete targets, comparing them to battleships after Pearl Harbor. Moriarty asserted that the US has provided inferior equipment to allies while adversaries like Russia, China, and Iran possess superior technology, making a US victory unlikely.
