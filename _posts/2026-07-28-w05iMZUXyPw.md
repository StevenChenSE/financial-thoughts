## Copper vs. Oil: A Binary Pair Trade

Tigre proposed a pair trade strategy centered on the uncertainty of the ongoing war. He acknowledged the long-term structural supply constraints and strong demand for copper, driven by factors beyond AI and electric vehicles. However, copper prices have already risen, making it less attractive as a "buy low" play at present.

The strategy hinges on a binary outcome:
1. **If the war heats up:** Oil prices will surge, and the "oil ship" will sail. However, this would negatively impact copper due to demand destruction and economic knock-on effects, creating a buying opportunity in copper.
2. **If the war unwinds:** Oil prices should drop, offering a discount entry. Meanwhile, copper may continue to perform well, meaning the "copper ship" sails.

Tigre emphasized that investors cannot participate in both scenarios simultaneously. "You can't kiss all the girls," he quoted mentor Doug Casey, advising against the urge to chase every opportunity. Instead, investors should wait for the specific conditions that align with their chosen asset.

"If I buy low, that could be 10x," Tigre said. "Whereas if I buy high... I could lose money." His final message was a call for discipline: do not rush to invest. Wait for Mr. Market to provide a clear, low-risk entry point.
