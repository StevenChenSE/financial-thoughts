## Volatility Catalysts and Technical Structures for the Trading Session

### Introduction

The market outlook for July 15, 2026, points to a day defined by a "cocktail of volatility" driven by multiple macroeconomic and corporate events. Traders are preparing for the release of Producer Price Index (PPI) data, another speech by Kevin Warsh, and the morning earnings report from ASML, a major semiconductor company. Given the current contraction in market structure, the analysis suggests exercising caution and waiting for clear signals before establishing major positions.

### Market Context and Catalysts

Today's session carries significant weight due to the convergence of high-impact events. Following yesterday's CPI release and bank earnings, the focus shifts to PPI data, which could further influence inflation expectations. Kevin Warsh is scheduled to speak again, adding to the potential for intraday turbulence. Additionally, ASML earnings will be released in the morning; as the first major semiconductor earnings report, this could set the tone for the sector and broader tech indices.

With markets in a contracted state, the prevailing advice is to avoid forming strong expectations until price action provides a definitive signal. The environment favors patience over aggression.

### Technical Structure

Technical analysis highlights positive developments on the daily and hourly charts, though confirmation is required.

*   **QQQ Daily Chart:** The QQQ index has finally established a higher low. The ideal bullish confirmation would be a higher high, specifically a break above last Friday's highs.
*   **NQ Pattern:** The Nasdaq-100 (NQ) is forming an inverse head and shoulder pattern. A breakout from this structure could trigger bullish action for the remainder of the week.
*   **Hourly Momentum:** NQ is currently consolidating above the 50 HMA (Hull Moving Average), which is viewed as a very positive sign. Yesterday's action saw price remain mostly above Monday's neutral range, indicating a lack of shorting opportunities and favoring bullish or choppy conditions.

### Trading Strategy and Timing

Volatility is expected to be front-loaded due to Kevin Warsh's morning speech. The analysis recommends avoiding the opening session. Instead, traders are advised to let Warsh finish speaking and wait for the lunch or afternoon session to trade. This approach is particularly prudent given the large overnight gap; while a gap fill is possible, it is unlikely to occur by the market open.

The preferred scenario involves price chopping during the morning session before breaking out, allowing for safer entries later in the day.

### Key Levels: NQ

The neutral range for NQ is established between **716** and **722**.

*   **Bullish Scenarios:**
    *   Holding above **722** keeps the bias positive.
    *   The next critical level is **725**, which aligns with the gap fill area. A direct break is doubted, but a move above this level opens the path to **728** and **731**.
    *   The best-case scenario is a morning chop followed by a break above **725**, enabling trades in the afternoon session.
*   **Bearish Scenarios:**
    *   A break below **716** increases the likelihood of a gap fill.
    *   Downside targets are **713**, followed by **710**.

### Key Levels: SPY

SPY is exhibiting a tighter contraction than QQQ. Yesterday, the index filled its gap, moved to the bottom of its range, and recovered to the top.

*   **Neutral Range:** **752.5** to **750.5**.
*   **Bullish Scenarios:**
    *   Above **752.5**, SPY could target **755** to fill the remaining gap, with further upside toward the **758** area.
*   **Bearish Scenarios:**
    *   Below **750.5**, support sits at **749**.
    *   A break of support likely leads to the gap fill at **745**.

### Summary

The trading plan for July 15 centers on navigating volatility from PPI data, Kevin Warsh's speech, and ASML earnings. Technicals show QQQ forming a higher low and NQ developing an inverse head and shoulder pattern, with consolidation above the 50 HMA supporting a cautiously bullish view. Traders should monitor NQ's neutral range of 716 to 722 and SPY's tight range of 750.5 to 752.5. The recommendation is to bypass the volatile morning session and look for breakout opportunities or gap fills during the lunch and afternoon hours.
