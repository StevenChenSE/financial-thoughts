## Gareth Soloway outlines critical support levels for crashing semiconductors, precious metals, and key market indices while identifying high-probability trade setups.

Gareth Soloway, Chief Market Strategist at Verified Investing, reports that the semiconductor sector has entered the collapse phase he warned of months ago. With the NASDAQ dropping approximately 2% and fear becoming pervasive, Soloway has exited the majority of his short positions and is now identifying buyable levels in major semiconductor names. While a total drawdown of 70% to 75% remains the projection for the cycle, traders should anticipate massive retracement bounces of 20% to 25%.

### Semiconductor Buy Zones and Oracle Contrarian Play

Soloway is beginning to "nibble" on long trades in semiconductors as they approach technical support. Key zones include:

*   **Micron:** A massive support zone exists between 800 and 750, including a gap fill. Soloway is considering longs if the price dips below 800.
*   **SanDisk:** Major support is identified between 1285 and 1200 following a breakdown and retrace pattern.
*   **Marvell:** The stock is approaching good support levels after a drop exceeding 40%.

Conversely, **Oracle** presents a contrarian opportunity. Soloway views Oracle as the inverse of the memory stock cycle; while memory stocks suffered from extreme euphoria, Oracle is facing extreme fear. With support around $120 formed by trend lines, Soloway sees potential in buying into the negative sentiment.

### Market Indices: S&P 500 and Nasdaq Risks

The broader market faces technical risks. The **S&P 500** is trading between major trend lines, and a break below current support could signal a failed breakout, which would be bearish for the overall market. In the **NASDAQ**, the critical level is 25,000. This "line in the sand" represents a pivot low. A break below 25,000 could accelerate the decline toward the next technical support at 24,000.

### Earnings Reactions and Key Stock Levels

Specific stocks are reacting sharply to earnings and news:

*   **Netflix:** The stock blew through support at 6870. The next technical level to watch is 64, which may offer a day-trade bounce opportunity.
*   **Intuitive Surgical:** Down on earnings, the key level is a gap fill at $330, where technical support and a potential bounce may occur.
*   **Alcoa:** Breaking lower from a bear flag formation, with support around $41 offering a potential swing trade setup.
*   **SpaceX:** Following a scrubbed launch, the stock dumped. Due to a lack of chart history, Soloway suggests waiting for a psychological level at $100 before considering a swing trade.

### Precious Metals: Silver and Gold Support

Silver is nearing Soloway's first target of 54, having fallen from highs above 100. He notes a shift in market psychology, with investors beginning to ask about buying at lower levels like 18 or 20. While this suggests the drop may not be over, Soloway plans to accumulate between 54 and 46. **Gold** has dropped to 3959, with support identified just below 3900.

### Commodities and Bitcoin Update

Geopolitical tensions between the US and Iran are pushing **Oil** higher. Resistance sits at 87, which Soloway identifies as a short level with a pullback target toward 70. **Natural Gas** is forming a bear flag; if the pattern continues without a recovery by mid-week, a breakdown is likely. **Bitcoin** has moved below the neckline of an inverse head and shoulders pattern, raising failure risks, though it remains a relative outperformer since early July.

### Summary

The market is characterized by increasing volatility and a shift from greed to fear in specific sectors. Soloway advises traders to capitalize on semiconductor bounces within defined support zones, monitor the Nasdaq 25,000 level closely, and watch for accumulation opportunities in silver as sentiment reaches extremes. With technical levels defining the path forward, discipline and chart-based analysis remain essential.
