The abrupt resignation of Jensen Huang as CEO of Nvidia marks the end of a 33-year era for one of the world’s most valuable technology companies. Following reports of personal misconduct and ongoing investigations into potential foreign intelligence links, Huang stepped down from his roles as president, CEO, and board member. ## The Timeline of Departure

Nvidia announced Huang’s resignation on Thursday morning, November 12th, though an SEC filing indicated the board accepted it effectively immediately on Wednesday, November 11th. The sequence of events unfolded rapidly over a single week:

*   **November 8:** Huang emailed Nvidia employees apologizing for being unfaithful to his wife regarding reports about his relationship with Alice Wang (also known as Hang Lee), an executive at Nvidia customer Mega Speed. He stated he was "fully committed" to the company.
*   **Same Day:** The Nvidia board launched an independent investigation into reports that Huang had been targeted by foreign intelligence operations, specifically Chinese agencies. Directors publicly expressed confidence in his leadership.
*   **November 10:** Independent directors brought in outside investigators, including former acting US attorney Jun Kim and the law firm Wachtell Lipton.
*   **November 11:** The *New York Times* published a report citing five sources regarding Huang’s relationship with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia’s Vice President of Healthcare, who reported directly to him. Simultaneously, the board accepted his resignation.

Reports from Reuters and the *Wall Street Journal* indicated that directors were preparing to remove Huang following the publication of the second affair. The official announcement gave no reason for his departure but thanked him for his contributions.
