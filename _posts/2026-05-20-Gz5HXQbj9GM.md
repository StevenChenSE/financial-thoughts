In a world racing toward digital financial dominance, one factor may determine the winners and losers more than any other: energy. That's the central argument from Eric Trump, co-founder of American Bitcoin, who believes America's abundant and affordable energy resources give it an insurmountable edge in both the Bitcoin mining and artificial intelligence competitions—and that a pro-crypto administration in Washington has positioned the country to lead a technological and financial revolution.

Speaking with hosts David Lynn and Bonnie Ch., Trump outlined why he believes the United States is uniquely positioned to dominate the digital asset space, how his company's cost-efficient mining operations are building something unprecedented, and why his personal journey into cryptocurrency was shaped by forces he never anticipated.

---

## America's Energy Independence: The Secret Weapon

The foundation of Trump's argument rests on a simple but critical point: Bitcoin mining and AI development are extraordinarily energy-intensive, and America has energy in abundance.

"Not only are we energy independent in America, we're actually exporting energy," Trump said. "Good luck if Europe ever wants to try and take on AI. Good luck if Europe ever wants to try and cryptocurrency. They can't do it because they don't have the energy profile to do it."

This energy advantage translates directly into competitive positioning. While other regions struggle with energy constraints, rising costs, and regulatory barriers, American mining operations—particularly in states like Texas—enjoy some of the lowest energy rates on Earth. The ability to mine Bitcoin at scale, reliably, and profitably creates a flywheel effect: more mining activity generates more revenue, which funds more infrastructure, which attracts more investment.

Trump was pointed in his comparisons: nations like the UK, which imports more energy than it produces, simply cannot compete in the race for AI supremacy or cryptocurrency dominance. "They don't stand a chance at the AI race," he said. "They don't stand a chance at the crypto race. They don't stand a chance at mining Bitcoin."

---

## The Policy Shift: From Hostility to Strategic Embrace

Beyond natural resources, the regulatory environment has undergone what Trump calls a "180-degree transformation" since his father entered office. The previous administration, he argued, treated cryptocurrency innovators as criminals rather than visionaries.

"Biden was literally throwing everybody in jail who dreamed about possibly being able to conduct finance better, more efficiently in a modern fashion," Trump said. "Now all of a sudden you've got a fair Fed chairman who's going to come in and be fantastic for the industry and actually understand the industry."

The anticipated appointment of Kevin Walsh as Federal Reserve chair represents, in Trump's view, a seismic shift. Walsh is described as the most tech-savvy Fed chair in history—someone who has explicitly stated that Bitcoin and AI will be the dominant drivers of the American economy.

"You had Biden, you had Kamala Harris who couldn't spell AI, and literally they didn't know what they were talking about," Trump continued. "They didn't care to understand it. You have my father who wants to win this race and be the best at it in the world."

This policy alignment extends throughout the administration. Trump's core argument: an administration that believes in winning attracts advisors and leaders who share that vision. "He wouldn't bring somebody into his inner circle if they didn't follow that premise."

The stakes, Trump emphasized, extend beyond national pride. "If we didn't do it, Asia would do it. If we didn't do it, the Middle East would do it." For him, American leadership isn't optional—it's imperative.

## Inside American Bitcoin: Building the Most Efficient Mining Operation in the World

Perhaps no company better illustrates Trump's ambitions than American Bitcoin, which went public just over eight months before this interview. The company's trajectory, Trump argues, demonstrates what's possible when American energy advantages meet disciplined capital allocation.

### Company Highlights at a Glance

The numbers tell a compelling story:

- **Bitcoin Holdings:** Well over 7,000 BTC in treasury
- **Mining Infrastructure:** Over 90,000 mining machines deployed
- **Compute Power:** More than 28 exahash of hashing capacity
- **Global Market Share:** Approximately 2.5–3% of total worldwide Bitcoin mining
- **Company Age:** Just 8 months and 3 days old at time of interview

"We have built a company from scratch in a company that's literally 8 months and 3 days old," Trump said. "It's an amazing story, and we're able to do it right here in America on American soil using American energy."

### The Cost Advantage: Mining Bitcoin at 50 Cents on the Dollar

What separates American Bitcoin from competitors isn't just scale—it's efficiency. Trump describes their mining cost structure in stark terms: the company can acquire Bitcoin through mining at roughly 50% of market value.

"Can you mine gold for 50 cents on the dollar?" Trump asked rhetorically. "What other kind of commodity, what other product can you mine at literally, roughly 50% on the dollar? And that's what we do at American Bitcoin every day because of our infrastructure, because of our low-cost structure, because of our energy profile."

This efficiency stems from several factors working in concert:

- **Strategic energy procurement** in low-cost jurisdictions
- **Tax incentives** for infrastructure investment, including bonus depreciation for capital-intensive facilities
- **Operational discipline** that keeps general and administrative costs minimal
- **Lean management structures** that avoid the excessive executive compensation prevalent in the industry

### The Strategy: Hold Everything

Unlike some competitors who sell mined Bitcoin to fund dividends or operations, American Bitcoin operates with a singular directive: accumulate and hold.

"Our only premise of our entire company is to grow our Bitcoin stack," Trump explained. "Our north star is growing Satoshi's per share."

When asked what would prompt a pivot toward selling Bitcoin, Trump was unequivocal: "It would have to be beyond catastrophic. Come hell or high water, it would take us a lot to have to sell our treasury. The only goal of our entire company is to grow that treasury."

This philosophy extends to executive compensation philosophy. Rather than paying hundreds of millions in cash compensation—money that could otherwise be invested in an appreciating asset—the company channels resources into its Bitcoin treasury. "When you're paying executives hundreds of millions of dollars to run a company, where that money could have otherwise been invested in the asset that's been growing at roughly 70% a year, year-over-year for the last decade, which do you think creates more shareholder value?"

## The Philosophy of "The Art of the Deal" Applied to Digital Assets

Trump's approach to business traces directly to principles articulated decades ago in his father's bestselling book. "I was born under the art of the deal," he said. "That book came out when I was very, very young, and I think it's been guiding principles in so many ways for my entire life."

### Core Principles in Practice

The Art of the Deal philosophy manifests in several observable ways at American Bitcoin:

1. **Negotiate everything.** From energy contracts to equipment purchases, every cost is scrutinized and negotiated.

2. **Run lean.** The company boasts the lowest SG&A (Selling, General & Administrative) expenses in the industry by a wide margin. "We probably have the lowest SG&A of any company in the industry. It's not even close. We can't even find competitors that are even in the same spectrum of cost structure and efficiency."

3. **Focus on asset accumulation.** Every dollar not spent on overhead is potentially another satoshi in the treasury.

4. **Be present.** Trump emphasizes walking the facilities, understanding operations intimately, and maintaining hands-on involvement.

This approach, Trump argues, isn't just about profitability—it's about competitive survival. "In the race of who can acquire the most the cheapest, we want to win. When you can mine something for 50 cents on the dollar, that's a remarkable business model."

## The Global Case for Bitcoin: Financial Freedom in Unstable Regions

While American Bitcoin focuses on domestic operations, Trump sees the broader cryptocurrency revolution as fundamentally global—and America as its greatest beneficiary.

Bitcoin's appeal is most acute in regions experiencing economic turmoil. "You look at so many places that have hyperinflation, that have bad banks, that have bad governments, that have instability in the markets, that have total corruption," Trump said. "What better place to own Bitcoin than those places where somebody can't seize your money in the middle of the night?"

### Breaking Down Barriers to Global Finance

Traditional cross-border transactions are cumbersome, slow, and expensive. Sending money to Morocco, for instance, requires finding a bank, navigating language barriers, managing currency conversion, and waiting days for settlement.

"Crypto eliminates all of that," Trump explained. "It allows instantaneous transfer of money all over the world."

This efficiency creates a compelling value proposition regardless of geopolitical stability:

- **Protection from currency collapse** in economies with unreliable monetary policy
- **Resistance to arbitrary seizure** by governments or financial institutions
- **24/7 global liquidity** accessible from any smartphone or tablet
- **Elimination of intermediary delays and fees**

### The Dollar's Dominance Amplifies America's Position

While Bitcoin provides global access to digital money, Trump sees the U.S. dollar as the ultimate beneficiary of this revolution.

"The largest benefactor of all of that is the United States because everybody wants the US dollar," he said. "You know what no one wants? They don't want the euro. They don't want any other currency. They want the dollar."

This creates a virtuous cycle: as digital asset adoption grows globally, demand for dollar-denominated stablecoins and dollar-pegged systems increases, reinforcing American monetary dominance rather than threatening it.

## The Personal Journey: From Real Estate Baron to Crypto Evangelist

Perhaps the most revealing part of the interview involved Trump's personal journey into cryptocurrency—a path he describes as both unexpected and transformative.

### Being "Debanked": A Catalyst for Change

Trump revealed that the family was systematically removed from traditional banking systems during politically motivated crackdowns. "We were debanked by the biggest banks in the country for doing nothing wrong. We own the best hotels, best residential buildings, best commercial buildings, the best real estate anywhere on Earth, and we were thrown out of so many of the biggest banks—the JP Morgans, the Capital Ones—like dogs. 300 accounts shut down overnight for all because of politics."

This experience, he argues, drove the family toward decentralized finance. "It drove us into digital assets. It drove us into the DeFi world. It drove us to meet people who were also being debanked by the very same people who were debanking us."

The shared experience created unexpected alliances. "That old analogy—the enemy of your enemy is your friend—That's exactly what happened between us and the crypto industry."

### Discovering a New Passion

Before the debanking experience, Trump was deeply entrenched in traditional hard assets—real estate, construction, tangible wealth. A transformation followed.

"If you would have told me five, six, seven years ago that I'd be this passionate about digital assets, I would have laughed," he admitted. "I literally would have laughed at you."

Today, that skepticism has transformed into unwavering conviction. "There's nothing I'm more passionate about. I love what I'm doing in American Bitcoin. I love what we're doing at World Liberty Financial. I love what we're doing with stablecoins. I love what we're doing in the entire ecosystem. And I could not be more proud of all of it."

## Family, Foundations, and Future Vision

The interview concluded with personal reflections that revealed the values underlying Trump's business philosophy.

### Childhood Lessons from Construction Sites

Trump's favorite childhood memories center on accompanying his father through construction sites as buildings rose from the ground.

"I loved walking the construction sites with him," he recalled. "Walking some of the most impressive buildings in New York as they were growing up—the steel and concrete, the superstructures. Being the fly on the wall during incredible meetings with tough New York Union contractors who were some of the best builders anywhere in the world."

These early experiences embedded lessons about tangible creation, quality craftsmanship, and the satisfaction of building something lasting—values he now applies to digital infrastructure.

### The Personal Relationship

Despite the demands of leadership, Trump describes a warm, enduring relationship with his father. "He still calls me honey—which is very funny. I'm a 42-year-old man with gray in my beard, and he still calls me honey."

He jokes that no matter how old his children become, "they'll always be Honey to me." The exchange underscores a philosophy of family continuity that extends from personal relationships into business succession and national leadership.

"I love this country. I'm proud of everything he's achieved, and I'm certainly proud to be one of the leaders in the digital asset race. Hopefully we'll be remembered for putting a lot of juice on this asset."

## Key Takeaways

1. **Energy determines digital destiny.** America's energy independence and export capacity give it a structural advantage in AI and cryptocurrency that other regions cannot match. Without abundant, affordable power, competitors will inevitably fall behind.

2. **Policy alignment amplifies natural advantages.** The shift from regulatory hostility to strategic embrace under the current administration has created conditions for American leadership. Appointments like the anticipated Fed chair signal long-term commitment to digital asset innovation.

3. **Efficiency beats scale.** American Bitcoin's strategy of mining at roughly 50% of market cost—through low energy prices, tax incentives, and operational discipline—creates sustainable competitive advantages that pure scale cannot replicate.

4. **Global adoption benefits America.** As Bitcoin provides financial freedom to billions in unstable regions, demand for dollar-denominated digital assets reinforces American monetary dominance rather than threatening it.

5. **Conviction compounds over time.** Trump's personal transformation—from skeptic to evangelist—demonstrates how understanding and conviction in digital assets can crystallize rapidly, particularly for those who have experienced the limitations of traditional financial systems.
