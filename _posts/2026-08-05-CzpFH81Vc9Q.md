## Certificate of the Week: Leontech Phoenix Memory Airbag Step Down

The featured product is a Phoenix Memory Airbag Step Down certificate issued by Leontech. It is currently trading near nominal value and offers a structure highly appreciated by investors, featuring a monthly premium of 1%, a capital protection barrier at 50%, and an Airbag option.

### Product Structure and Underlying Basket
*   **Duration:** Maximum 4 years.
*   **Underlying Basket:** The certificate tracks four major banking stocks:
    *   **Italian:** Banco BPM and Intesa Sanpaolo.
    *   **European:** Commerzbank and Société Générale.
*   **Current Performance:** Société Générale is the best performer in the basket with a gain of 9.40%, while Commerzbank is slightly negative.

### Returns and Early Redemption
The certificate pays a monthly coupon of 1% until the barrier is breached, utilizing the Memory effect if necessary.
*   **First Early Redemption Date:** The 3rd month of the product's life (October 8).
*   **Trigger:** Starts at 100% and steps down by 1% monthly.
*   **Guarantee:** Even in a positive scenario leading to early redemption, investors are guaranteed at least three coupons of €10 each.
*   **Annualized Yield:** Approximately 11.76% (47.24% over the maximum 4-year term), assuming a purchase price slightly above par.

### Capital Protection and Airbag Option
The certificate features robust downside protection:
*   **Barrier:** Positioned at 50% of the initial value.
*   **Airbag (Low Strike):** Also positioned at 50%.
*   **Loss Calculation:** In the event the barrier is breached at maturity, losses are calculated starting from the 50% level, not zero.
*   **Buffer:** Investors have a protection buffer of up to 48% on the weakest title in the basket (currently Commerzbank).
*   **Scenario Analysis:** In a worst-case scenario where the underlying drops 50% and stays there, the capital loss is limited to approximately 4% (redeeming roughly €960-950). Crucially, coupons are detached from capital protection; therefore, receiving 24-36 coupons over time would result in a positive overall return despite the capital shortfall.

### Basket Dynamics and Dividends
The high dividend yields of Banco BPM and Intesa Sanpaolo support the certificate's structure, while Société Générale offers a decent yield. Commerzbank has a lower dividend yield but provides necessary diversification with lower correlation to the Italian titles, enabling the attractive 12% annualized return and the 50% barrier with Airbag.
