## Chris Vermeulen reveals critical technical signals suggesting equities, gold, and Bitcoin are all vulnerable to significant declines

The market rally that defined 2025 is showing clear signs of exhaustion. According to Chris Vermeulen, chief market strategist at TheTechnicalTraders.com, multiple asset classes—including stocks, gold, and Bitcoin—are now pointing toward substantial downside. In a recent market outlook discussion, Vermeulen outlined technical indicators and price patterns that suggest investors should prepare for what could be one of the most significant market corrections in recent memory.

"What to pick to get ahead" was the dominant question during last year's euphoric market conditions. Now, Vermeulen argues, the more pressing question is whether investors should be in the markets at all. His analysis, rooted in technical analysis and market internals, suggests a prudent, cautious approach is warranted as key indicators shift from bullish to bearish configurations.

## Reading the Market's Warning Signs

Vermeulen emphasizes that successful market navigation requires understanding what big money is doing rather than simply following price action. His daily S&P 500 charts are now displaying orange bars—a significant departure from the green bars that dominated during the recent rally. This color shift represents a critical change in market internals, signaling that institutional money is beginning to rotate out of equities and into safety positions.

"When we look at the market internals, big money flows, sentiment is changing. It's going from green bars, which is big money is moving in, buying and holding, and now we're starting to see big money move away. They're looking for safety," Vermeulen explained during the discussion.

The warning signs extend beyond just stock market internals. When utilities begin to outperform the broader equity market, it historically signals that money is preparing to flow out of stocks. Combined with a dollar breakout—another indicator that investors are seeking safe-haven assets—these technical signals paint a concerning picture for equity markets in the near term.

## S&P 500: The 18-24% Correction Scenario

Using Fibonacci retracement analysis on the monthly chart, Vermeulen identifies the S&P 500 as vulnerable to a substantial pullback. Based on the significant rally that has already occurred, his technical analysis suggests the index could decline to approximately 5,500 to 6,000—a potential 18 to 24 percent correction from current levels.

For short-term traders, the picture is even more immediate. A shorter-term Fibonacci analysis indicates a potential pullback to around 7,000 to 7,100 on the S&P 500, representing a 3.5 to 6 percent decline—a typical healthy correction within an established uptrend. However, Vermeulen cautions that if the market fails to find support at these levels, the deeper 18-24 percent scenario becomes increasingly likely.

"Utilities start to outperform the stock market, it's a big red flag. We need to be aware that money is about to start flowing out of stocks and we could start to see the equities market collapse," Vermeulen warned.

His current positioning reflects this cautious outlook. While still maintaining a small sliver of equity exposure, Vermeulen has closed out his QQQ positions near the peak and reduced S&P 500 holdings significantly. He is prepared to either hold cash earning interest or pivot to inverse ETFs or dollar-focused instruments if the market breaks down as anticipated.

## Gold's Vulnerability: 17% Downside Potential

Even precious metals, which have been a beacon of strength, are not immune to the broader market rotation. Vermeulen's analysis of the gold chart points to a potential pullback to the $3,300 to $3,600 range—representing approximately 17 percent downside from current levels. This would constitute a meaningful correction in an asset class that has attracted significant safe-haven flows throughout the recent market volatility.

The gold analysis comes at a time when the metal has already experienced substantial gains, making it susceptible to profit-taking pressures if broader market conditions deteriorate.

## Bitcoin: A Bleak Technical Picture

Perhaps most alarming is Vermeulen's analysis of Bitcoin's technical picture. The cryptocurrency, which has already declined more than 50 percent from its all-time highs, is now trading below the critical psychological threshold of $60,000. From a technical perspective, Vermeulen sees little evidence that a bottom is imminent.

"Bitcoin is very bearish. It is breaking down at this point. Money has been flowing out of the crypto space for a while," Vermeulen stated.

His analysis points to $44,000 as the next critical downside target for Bitcoin—a level that would break the 2024 lows and represent approximately another 17 percent decline from current prices. However, the longer-term monthly chart suggests even darker days ahead, with potential targets as low as $16,000 based on similar chart patterns observed in previous market cycles.

The bearish technical setup for Bitcoin is reinforced by the asset's price position relative to its 150-day moving average. When shorter-term moving averages fail to hold above the longer-term average and both slope downward, it confirms a bearish environment where rallies become opportunities for selling rather than sustainable trend continuations.

## The Capital Rotation Pattern

Vermeulen identifies a clear pattern of capital rotation that has unfolded over recent months. During one particular phase, money flowed aggressively from Bitcoin into gold and silver as investors sought the perceived safety of precious metals. Now, that rotation appears to be continuing, with capital moving out of gold and into technology and artificial intelligence-related names such as Micron.

"This whole phase right through here, that is when gold and silver were rocketing higher. And it just goes to show that people in the crypto space and investing in general, but in crypto, you're going for performance, right? You're going for like quick retirement ticket, like it's big performance. That's what people generally are chasing for it. And as soon as something becomes shinier than Bitcoin, which pardon the pun, was precious metals, money came out of Bitcoin and it just piled into gold," Vermeulen explained.

This rotation pattern suggests the crypto space has entered a prolonged bearish environment where investor enthusiasm has shifted elsewhere. Without fresh capital inflows and with existing holders continuing to exit, the path of least resistance for Bitcoin appears to be lower.

## Strategy for Navigating Uncertainty

Vermeulen's approach to the current market environment prioritizes capital preservation over aggressive positioning. His trading methodology relies on statistical analysis of historical price behavior to identify levels where markets typically experience exhaustion. When these thresholds are reached, he scales out of positions systematically rather than waiting for certainty that never comes.

"We scale out as we go up. We had multiple different targets and that was one of the key target levels. That's how we gauge it. It's based on statistical analysis. What does the index do? How does it typically move over time?" Vermeulen described.

This disciplined approach recognizes that markets rarely offer perfect certainty. Instead, successful investing requires acting when odds are dramatically in your favor and stepping aside when the probability landscape becomes less clear. Currently, Vermeulen's indicators suggest the market is in "no man's land"—neutral territory where the outcome could resolve in either direction.

"When the market doesn't know what it wants to do, we're generally our systems generally moving us out of the position, lightening our portfolio because it becomes closer and closer to a coin toss. I don't want to win on coin tosses. I want to win when the odds are dramatically in my favor," he stated.

## Key Takeaways for Investors

The confluence of warning signs across multiple asset classes—utilities outperforming stocks, the dollar breaking out, orange market internals replacing green, and Bitcoin's breakdown—suggests the risk-reward environment for equity exposure has deteriorated significantly. Vermeulen's analysis points to potential pullbacks of 18-24 percent for stocks, 17 percent for gold, and substantially more for Bitcoin from current levels.

For investors, the strategic implications are clear: maintain disciplined risk management, consider reducing equity exposure, and prepare capital for deployment at significantly lower prices. Whether the market ultimately confirms these technical signals remains to be seen, but the technical evidence suggests caution is warranted. As Vermeulen advises, "The trends need to come to an end before we jump ship"—but the evidence increasingly suggests that end may be approaching.
