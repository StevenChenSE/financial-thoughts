## A Grim Prediction for the Global Economy

Todd "Bubba" Horwitz, founder of BubbaTrading.com, warns that the financial markets are setting up for another crisis akin to 2008. Despite recent rallies, Horwitz remains bearish, citing record-high mortgage defaults, excessive consumer debt, and an unsustainable level of overall economic leverage. In this discussion, he outlines his current short positions, his views on inflation and Federal Reserve policy, and identifies the safest places to hide capital during the impending downturn.

## The Oil Market and Inflation Realities

Horwitz remains short on oil, arguing that geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, such as the Iran conflict, will not sustain high prices due to a worldwide glut. He predicts WTI crude could drop to the high $50s or low $60s by year-end. While government data suggests cooling inflation, Horwitz argues that real-world costs for gas, food, and delivery remain high, creating severe stress for consumers who are already deep in debt.

## Tech Selloff and the Danger of Leverage

The semiconductor sector is facing pressure, with Horwitz expecting further downside for stocks like NVIDIA, Intel, and Qualcomm. He draws parallels between current parabolic moves and the dot-com bubble of 2000. Horwitz strongly criticizes the use of leveraged ETFs, citing a massive crash in South Korea where 1.2 million retail accounts faced margin calls. He emphasizes that leverage should only be used for short-term trades with strict stop-losses, as time decay and volatility can wipe out accounts quickly.

## Fed Policy and Interest Rates

Horwitz believes the Federal Reserve is forced to hike interest rates to combat persistent inflation, regardless of the impact on the weak consumer. He notes a significant divergence between the Fed funds rate and the 10-year Treasury note, which has risen sharply. He predicts a rate hike could occur as early as September 2026, driven by market forces rather than Fed preference. This dynamic benefits banks but increases borrowing costs for mortgages and retail loans.

## Safe Havens: Gold, Silver, and the Dollar

Amidst the uncertainty, Horwitz is bullish on precious metals. He has been buying gold near $4,000, silver around $55, and platinum at $1,600, viewing these levels as solid bases for a steady uptrend. He also expects the US dollar to strengthen due to global uncertainty and its status as a safe-haven reserve currency, potentially reaching 102–103 on the index. Horwitz advises investors to define their risk, hedge their portfolios, and prepare for a potential 40–60% market correction.
