## The End of the Silver Suffering

Silver investors can exhale: the long bear market is officially over. After weeks of gold outperforming silver with stronger technical signals, silver has now taken the lead. According to Daniel Wilhelmi of Hot Trades of Day, recent price action confirms a bearish-to-bullish reversal and the start of a new rally phase—before most of the market has even noticed.

The message is clear: every correction in silver and silver miners from here on out should be viewed as a buying opportunity, not a reason to wait for a miracle drop.

## Key Technical Signals in Silver

The silver spot chart now shows two of the most important early bullish signals traders look for:

- Break of the long-term downtrend: The downtrend that began in January 2020 was decisively broken, with confirmation in the days following the initial breakout. This is a classic end-of-bear-market signal.
- Reclaim of the 50-day moving average: Silver jumped back above its 50-day MA (around $61.90–$62.00), a level it had only briefly touched in March before falling back. Since the summer selloff and June–July consolidation, price had stayed below this line.

Silver remains below the 200-day moving average for now. Crossing above that level is expected to be the catalyst that brings “Main Street” attention back to silver and silver mining stocks.

Wilhelmi stresses that this is still early. The medium-term downtrend channel break was already behind us; the long-term trend break is the new, more significant development.

## Why This Reversal Matters

Missing the start of a new rally is one of the most common—and costly—mistakes investors make. By the time sentiment shifts and everyone agrees the market has turned, the best companies have already run higher. Latecomers are often left with mediocre or problematic names that lag for a reason.

Wilhelmi uses a simple analogy: show up early to a club and you have choices; show up late and only the leftovers remain. In markets, that translates to:

- Early buyers: quality companies at good prices.
- Late buyers: lower-quality companies, or high-quality ones at stretched valuations.

The goal now is not to chase the absolute lowest price from weeks ago but to buy strong companies at reasonable prices with a high-probability setup behind them.

## What Could Still Push Silver Lower?

The reversal isn’t a guarantee of a straight line up. Wilhelmi outlines only extreme scenarios that would likely break silver lower from here:

- A severe oil price shock.
- A sharp, sustained rise in the U.S. dollar that pressures gold and then silver.
- A major dislocation in the bond market.

Absent those, the base case is continued upside with periodic pullbacks that offer entry points.

## Silver’s Key Support Levels and the “Retest”

After a major breakout, it is common for price to return to the broken level to confirm it as new support. This is known as a retest.

For silver, multiple support factors align:

- 50-day moving average: Around $61.90–$62.00; the most important near-term level.
- Broken long-term downtrend: A retest zone roughly around $60.
- Short-term uptrend: In place since mid-July, also providing support near $60.
- Horizontal support zone: Roughly around $63.

Wilhelmi expects a likely retest into the $60–$62 range. Watching how silver reacts at the 50-day MA will be critical. A bounce there would be another strong bullish signal.

These are not buy recommendations, but examples of how different types of silver stocks are positioned as the bear market ends.

First Majestic is a senior producer that already looks stronger than the silver spot chart:

- Long-term downtrend: Broken.
- 50-day and 200-day MAs: Above the 50-day and approaching the 200-day.
- Uptrend: Intact, with clear downside protection.

A key feature of First Majestic’s chart is the “mother of all retests.” After a major breakout from a long-term base, price pulled back and retested the breakout zone before resuming higher—exactly the pattern Wilhelmi highlighted for silver itself.

Potential retest levels:

Wilhelmi notes that First Majestic has high silver price leverage among large producers and plenty of upside gaps to fill. The main caveat: the company should pursue growth projects or acquisitions to secure future expansion.

Aya Gold & Silver is another familiar name from the channel, and its chart is impressive:

Wilhelmi personally missed buying Aya earlier as he waited for weakness that never came. He wouldn’t chase it after this run but acknowledges it as a top momentum play with significant upside potential if the silver super cycle continues.

### 3. Argenta Silver – Explorer with Limited Upside for Now

Argenta Silver is an explorer with a large silver project in Argentina and a popular name among silver investors. Wilhelmi met the management and believes they are competent, but remains cautious:

- Still trading within its long-term downtrend.
- Upside is blocked relatively close above by the 200-day MA and trendline.
- Early-stage project requiring substantial capital, implying likely dilution.

While the project looks promising, Wilhelmi argues the risk-reward is less attractive compared to other silver stocks right now. He expects more dilution and sees limited short-term upside relative to peers.

### 4. Santacruz Silver – Turnaround Story with High Silver Leverage

Santacruz Silver is a turnaround story in South America with a silver project that is highly sensitive to the silver price:

- Already broke above the 200-day MA ahead of the broader market.
- Above the 50-day MA and with downtrends broken.
- Demonstrates strong relative strength and dynamic moves when silver rises.

The company has successfully reduced costs, though they are still not as low as typical Latin American peers. As silver prices increase, Santacruz’s profits can jump quickly, making it an attractive swing trade if you believe in higher silver. Wilhelmi, a disclosed shareholder, notes that long-term growth will eventually require additional projects or acquisitions.

## Strategy Going Forward

The core themes from this analysis are:

- The silver bear market is over based on clear technical signals.
- Corrections should be used to buy quality miners and explorers on dips.
- Strength leads strength: early in a new cycle, the strongest companies get bought first.
- Avoid falling in love with past lows; focus on current price versus future potential.
- Watch silver’s behavior at the 50-day MA and the $60–$62 retest zone for confirmation of continued upside.

Wilhelmi wraps up by reiterating that while prices may not be at the absolute lows of a few weeks ago, the combination of attractive current levels and a high-probability bullish setup makes now a strategic time to position for the next leg up in silver.
