A notable market analyst has issued a bold forecast for silver, predicting the precious metal will "challenge the three-digit level" this year, with a sustained target around **$100** over the next twelve months.

The analysis breaks down the potential path upward, noting that past rallies have followed a distinct pattern. "The movement from 30 to 40 was very methodical. It took some time," the expert observes, "but the movement from 40 to 60 and then from 80 to 120 was very very fast." This acceleration was highlighted on the chart from late November through January's peak.

Recent price action is viewed as a healthy consolidation. After trading near **$96** before a period of market volatility—referred to as "the war of silver"—prices have retracted to around **$80**. The expert believes this pullback is constructive: "It was good for silver to have a little bit more retraction so that way we can solidify and have more of a tested level as opposed to this wild ride."

Looking ahead, the near term may see increased turbulence. "I think in the next month or so, we would anticipate a lot more volatility and confirmation," the analysis states. However, the longer-term outlook remains firmly bullish, underpinned by strong fundamentals.

The concluding forecast is clear: "But mid to long term, I would say for the next year, our forecast is around the **$100**." This projection suggests that after a period of consolidation and testing, silver could be poised for another rapid upward leg toward a historic three-digit valuation.
