## The New Bull Market Has Arrived

Jason Yanowitz, co-founder of Blockworks, has declared the start of a new bull market in the cryptocurrency industry. In a recent interview, Yanowitz outlined a bullish outlook, predicting that Bitcoin will cross the $200,000 mark within the next 18 months. He argues that the current rally is not a temporary "head fake" or a short squeeze, but a fundamental shift driven by improved macroeconomic conditions and a maturing on-chain economy.

## Macro Triggers and the Debasement Trade

The catalyst for the recent price surge was a significant shift in U.S. Treasury policy. On August 19, the Treasury announced it would double its long-end bond buybacks from $2 billion to $4 billion per operation. This move revived the "debasement trade," a strategy where investors seek hard assets to protect against currency devaluation.

Yanowitz notes that this policy change triggered a record short liquidation event, with $2.7 billion in short positions liquidated in a single day. This forced buying ignited a 20% rally, pushing Bitcoin from the $60,000–$65,000 range to over $80,000. Mainstream financial media, including Reuters, explicitly tied the move to a softer dollar and renewed fears of currency debasement, signaling that traditional capital allocators are returning to crypto.

## The On-Chain Economy: Stablecoins and Hyperliquid

While Bitcoin's price action captures headlines, Yanowitz emphasizes that the real story is the growth of the on-chain economy. He highlights three key metrics:

1.  **Stablecoin Supply:** Stablecoins have reached $300 billion in supply, acting as the "cash balance" of the on-chain economy. Yanowitz argues that stablecoin supply is a more reliable indicator of industry health than Bitcoin's price, similar to how website creation outpaced the dot-com bubble crash.
2.  **Solana Activity:** Solana has hit an all-time high of $4.2 billion in non-vote transactions monthly, with 24 million trades per day. The median fee is now just 0.004 cents, making it a highly efficient venue for trading.
3.  **Hyperliquid's Breakout:** Hyperliquid is the first venue to successfully break out of crypto into mainstream markets. It generated $150 million in quarterly revenue with only 12–13 employees. Notably, 29% of its daily volume ($2.75 billion) is in Real World Assets (RWAs), including equities, commodities, and FX. This demonstrates that crypto is no longer just about digital assets but is becoming the infrastructure for global finance.

## Bitcoin Treasury Companies: Winners and Losers

The interview also addressed the performance of Bitcoin treasury companies. Many have lost $80 billion in value since mid-2024, with some turning to net sellers. Yanowitz argues that these companies are struggling because their underlying assets (Bitcoin) have been volatile. However, he points to Hyperliquid Strategies as a counterexample, a treasury company holding $2.4 billion in Hyperliquid that has performed well. He suggests that treasury companies holding high-quality, revenue-generating assets like Hyperliquid or Solana will outperform those holding only Bitcoin.

## The Future of Crypto: Tokenization and Consolidation

Yanowitz believes the next phase of crypto will be defined by the tokenization of all traditional assets. "Dollars are becoming stablecoins, stocks are becoming tokens, and exchanges are becoming protocols," he says. He predicts that every stock, bond, currency, and commodity will eventually be tokenized, with data providers powering these markets.

He also forecasts a period of consolidation and M&A in the industry. Blockworks, which recently acquired Misari, is positioning itself as a consolidator, aiming to become the "Moody's or S&P of the on-chain economy." With a current valuation of $200 million, Yanowitz sees a path to a $120 billion business, mirroring the scale of traditional credit rating agencies.

While Bitcoin may not hit $100,000 immediately, the structural changes in the market suggest a sustained bull run, with $200,000 a realistic target within 18 months.
