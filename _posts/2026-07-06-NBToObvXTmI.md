## Ryan King, EVP of Capital Markets at Equinox Gold, analyzes the macro forces driving hard assets and shares insights on the company's transformative merger with Orla Mining.

Ryan King, EVP of Capital Markets at Equinox Gold, returned to discuss the structural bull market in hard assets, the inevitability of a global sovereign debt crisis, and Equinox Gold's strategic growth through its merger with Orla Mining. King outlined how monetary policy and fiscal deficits are devaluing fiat currencies, supporting a long-term gold price target of $17,000 per ounce, while detailing how Equinox is consolidating into a diversified senior producer with significant exposure to tier-one jurisdictions.

### The Macro Case for Gold and the Debt Reckoning

King emphasized that the current environment is defined by the devaluation of fiat currencies against hard assets, including gold, silver, copper, and real estate. This trend is driven by years of quantitative easing and loose monetary policy that have accumulated the largest debt levels in history.

"In my opinion, we had a series of events that finally really stacked up against hard assets," King explained. "We're seeing the devaluation of fiat currencies against all hard major assets."

Referencing predictions from prominent investor Pierre Lassonde, King noted, "Just on the M2 money supply alone, we're talking about $17,000 plus dollars per ounce. And that's his prediction over the next 5 years."

King drew parallels to the 1970s, when gold rose from $180 to $850 an ounce amid policy uncertainty and geopolitical shocks. He acknowledged that while gold has corrected from highs near $5,500 to approximately $4,000, the structural integrity of the bull market remains intact. He also cited Martin Armstrong's forecasting, which suggests a potential pullback to the $3,800–$4,000 range before a resumption of the upward trend driven by the overarching sovereign debt crisis.

### Global Fiscal Pressures and Currency Devaluation

The discussion highlighted the unsustainable debt trajectories across major economies. King pointed to Congressional Budget Office projections indicating the U.S. debt-to-GDP ratio will exceed 120% by 2036, surpassing World War II levels. Similar pressures are evident in Japan, where fiscal deficits are widening and military spending is doubling, and in the UK, where social welfare spending has recently outpaced tax receipts.

"Central banks are faced with having to raise rates but are challenged to do so because they cannot afford the higher interest costs on the amounts of debt they have," King stated. "We're heading towards a major sovereign debt crisis again."

King noted that politicians often prioritize short-term GDP growth and re-election by continuing to print money, pushing the inevitable reckoning down the road. He referenced Ray Dalio's observations on the lifecycle of empires, suggesting that while the timeline for this debt reset is uncertain, the pressure on financial systems will persist.

### Equinox Gold's Strategic Transformation with Orla Mining

Amid the macro backdrop, Equinox Gold is executing a significant strategic transformation through its merger with Orla Mining. Following a previous merger with Calibre Mining, the deal with Orla positions Equinox to become a senior gold producer with an annualized pro forma production guidance midpoint of 1.1 million ounces.

The combined entity maintains a focused portfolio across Canada, the United States, and Mexico, with 70% to 75% of the Net Asset Value (NAV) derived from assets in Canada and the U.S. King emphasized that the merger does not add jurisdictional complexity, instead consolidating operations in safe, tier-one jurisdictions favored by institutional investors.

Leadership for the combined company will include Darren Hall as CEO, Jason Simpson as President, and Chuck Jeannes as Chairman. Jeannes, former CEO of Goldcorp, brings extensive experience from the last gold cycle, adding depth to the management team.

### Investor Sentiment, Valuation, and Shareholder Strength

King addressed the current shift in market sentiment, noting that enthusiasm has cooled as gold corrects and investors worry about buying the top. However, he views the current valuation as a compelling opportunity. Equinox Gold is trading at approximately 0.5 times NAV, a significant discount compared to peers trading at 7 to 8 times NAV.

"The stock is down over 30% to 35% from its top, but the underlying business has transformed significantly," King said. He highlighted the strength of the pro forma shareholder base, which includes mining legends Ross Beaty and Pierre Lassonde, as well as Prem Watsa of Fairfax. Together, these industry veterans hold approximately 11% of the pro forma company, signaling strong insider confidence.

### M&A Strategy and Acquisition Criteria

When evaluating potential acquisitions, King outlined three critical criteria: jurisdiction, economics, and growth potential.

1.  **Jurisdiction:** Equinox prioritizes assets in stable, tier-one regions to mitigate political and operational risks.
2.  **Economics:** King stressed the importance of demonstrating solid returns at conservative gold price assumptions. "We look at a much lower gold price environment, somewhere between $2,200 and $2,500 an ounce, to see if that can demonstrate a solid positive return on invested capital."
3.  **Growth:** The company seeks assets with the potential to expand beyond known resources, supporting long-term sustainability and production growth.

King noted that Equinox spends over $100 million annually on exploration to discover new zones and extend mine life, ensuring the portfolio remains robust regardless of short-term price fluctuations.

### Outlook and Execution

Looking ahead, King anticipates that cycles may be shortening due to geopolitical tensions and debt dynamics, leading to potential revaluations of hard assets. For Equinox, the focus remains on disciplined capital allocation, reliability, and delivering on production guidance.

"Reliability is critical," King concluded. "Delivering into expectations, on time and on budget, is the critical path moving forward. With our leadership team and diversified portfolio, we are well-positioned to unlock value for shareholders in this environment."
