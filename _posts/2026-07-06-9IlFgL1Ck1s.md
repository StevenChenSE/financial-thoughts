## Market Stress Test and Geopolitical Scenarios

Global markets are currently facing a pivotal stress test driven by escalating tensions between the United States and Iran. The coming seven to eleven days represent a critical window that could determine the trajectory of nuclear inspections, the viability of diplomatic negotiations, and the risk of military escalation. While headlines cycle rapidly, the economic implications are profound, particularly regarding oil prices, inflation, and the flow of shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.

Investors are monitoring three distinct scenarios that will dictate market behavior:

*   **Worst-Case Scenario:** Diplomatic negotiations fail completely, nuclear inspections are permanently rejected, and military options gain traction. This outcome could trigger a classic risk-off environment, pushing oil prices above $80 and exerting significant pressure on interest-rate-sensitive technology stocks.
*   **Middle Scenario (Most Likely):** Diplomacy enters a phase of delaying tactics with no clear agreement or outright rejection. This path suggests a volatile, sideways market where nerves remain frayed, but an immediate crash is avoided.
*   **Best-Case Scenario:** Tehran unexpectedly yields, allowing inspections to begin and de-escalation to take hold. In this case, the geopolitical risk premium could vanish rapidly, potentially leading to a strong stock market recovery, though gold might face continued downward pressure.

## Market Sentiment and Key Indicators

Current market data reflects heightened anxiety. The CNN Fear & Greed Index is hovering around 25, placing sentiment firmly in the "Extreme Fear" zone. Historically, a reading of 25 often signals a turning point, as market bottoms tend to form when pessimism is most widespread. However, caution is warranted due to specific sub-indicators.

Safe haven demand has dropped to -4%, indicating that government bonds are significantly outperforming stocks, a clear sign of a flight to safety. Additionally, the S&P 500 is approaching its 125-day moving average, and the VIX is rising steadily, though it remains below the panic threshold of 40. These signals suggest the market is tense and that a final sell-off may not yet have occurred.

## Strategic Asset Analysis

Amidst this uncertainty, a disciplined approach focuses on maintaining a core position in the ACWI IMI ETF while selectively targeting individual opportunities. The following four assets are currently under close watch:

### 1. Alphabet (Google)
Alphabet has faced headwinds due to US antitrust concerns and fears regarding AI competitors like Perplexity and OpenAI challenging its search monopoly. However, the fundamentals remain robust. The stock trades at a price-to-earnings ratio of approximately 25, which is attractive relative to historical levels. Google Cloud demonstrated exceptional growth, increasing revenue by 63% in the first quarter of 2026 to around $20 billion, with significant improvements in profitability. YouTube continues to dominate the video market. While regulatory and technological risks persist, the valuation presents a compelling entry point for a high-quality company.

### 2. Micron Technology
As the AI boom accelerates, the demand for memory chips is undergoing a structural shift. Micron is a critical "pick-and-shovel" play for this sector, supplying the memory required for Nvidia's chips. The company is now delivering HBM 4 in large volumes to key customers and has provided samples to others, with HBM 4 I expected to enter mass production in 2027. Following a decline from all-time highs, Micron's valuation has become more attractive. Due to its high cyclicality, the stock could react strongly if market conditions stabilize, making it a strategic hold for AI exposure.

### 3. Rocket Lab
Rocket Lab stands out as a speculative favorite in the aerospace sector. While SpaceX dominates headlines, Rocket Lab has established itself as a leading commercial launch provider with its Electron rocket and is preparing the first flight of its reusable medium-lift Neutron rocket for the fourth quarter of 2026. The company is generating record revenue from launches and space systems, though it remains unprofitable on an adjusted EBITDA basis. Investors should anticipate high volatility, technical risks, and potential delays. Despite these factors, the company's diversification and long-term potential make it a high-risk, high-reward position worth accumulating on price declines.

### 4. Gold
Gold has recently faced pressure, testing the psychological level of $4,000. This decline is attributed to a stronger dollar and expectations of further US interest rate increases, which make non-yielding assets less attractive compared to government bonds. However, gold retains its role as a crucial diversification tool. If the Iran situation escalates, gold could quickly reclaim its status as a safe haven. Furthermore, global central bank demand remains robust, with China continuing to expand its gold reserves. The current dip is being monitored as a potential entry point for portfolio insurance, though it is not viewed as a primary wealth-generation asset.
