## Mining Stocks: Selectivity is Key

When discussing mining stocks, Hoese emphasizes the importance of selectivity. He warns against simply buying broad miner ETFs or developers. Instead, investors should focus on companies with **high ore grades**. 

*   **High Ore Grades:** These companies have lower production costs and will see their margins expand significantly as commodity prices rise. They are the "free cash flow" generators of the bull market.
*   **Low Ore Grades:** New mines with low ore grades will have thin margins and will effectively set the floor for commodity prices, but they won't offer the same explosive growth as high-grade producers.
*   **Royalty Companies:** While they perform well, their valuations are often already high, offering less upside than selectively chosen producers.
