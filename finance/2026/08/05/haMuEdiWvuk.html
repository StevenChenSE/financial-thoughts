Macro Disconnect: Oil, the Dollar, and Global Monetary Reordering

Landy highlighted an unusual divergence between oil prices and the U.S. dollar index (DXY). Historically, rising oil correlates with a stronger dollar, but recent months have shown oil declining while DXY gained. This disconnect reflects broader shifts in global monetary dynamics.

He referenced Scott Bessent’s 2024 writings on global monetary reordering, suggesting we are now in a transitional phase where traditional correlations are breaking down. Landy noted that the Fed’s ideal scenario—lower oil prices combined with a stronger dollar—is typically contradictory but may be emerging due to these structural shifts.
