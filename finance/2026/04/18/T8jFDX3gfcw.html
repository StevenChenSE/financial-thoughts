That repositioning is already underway. Several significant developments have emerged in the days since the Islamabad negotiations:

This signals movement toward full military cooperation, which would give America control over the Strait of Malacca—the world’s most critical chokepoint.

The Pentagon has asked Detroit’s General Motors and Ford to begin war munitions manufacturing. America, it seems, is preparing for a long war of attrition.

Naval Blockade Strategy: Trump has announced a naval blockade against Iran. Rather than risking ships near the Strait of Hormuz—where they’d be within range of Iranian ballistic missiles and drone strikes—the Americans are instead embargoing ships in the Indian Ocean. This effectively means America is imposing a worldwide blockade against any vessels it deems breaking sanctions, potentially including Russian, Chinese, and Iranian shipping.

“What America is really doing is saying that we are going to impose a worldwide blockade against any enemy ships that we deem breaking sanctions,” Professor Jen noted.
