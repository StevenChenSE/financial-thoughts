Introduction

Bitcoin is currently attempting to form a local top in the present price region, exhibiting selling pressure over the last few days. The central question for investors is whether this marks the peak of “wave two” following the July rally, or if a more significant sell-off is imminent. This analysis explores the Elliott wave count, Bitcoin dominance trends, and the implications for the altcoin market, suggesting that while Bitcoin may be near a bear market low, the path forward remains complex.

Bitcoin Wave Structure and Resistance

From a daily timeframe perspective, Bitcoin appears close to a bear market low. While some selling pressure may persist for a few more months, the majority of the decline is likely complete. However, the final downward move is expected to be less straightforward than many anticipate.

The current “wave two” correction could potentially extend into August. This means traders expecting a direct, sharp sell-off might be surprised if the market consolidates within the current range for a longer period. The overarching wave structure remains an ABC decline, with the third phase of the bear market beginning in May. The current price action represents the “C wave” decline.

Key Technical Levels:

  • Resistance: A decisive break above $76,600 is required to invalidate the primary bearish scenario. This level aligns with the trendline and the 200-day moving average. As long as this resistance holds, downward pressure remains.
  • Support: The critical support level is around $56,500. This corresponds to the 38.2% Fibonacci retracement and serves as the first major support on the chart.

It is normal for Bitcoin to rally into resistance, potentially forming a top in early August. Historically, July is bullish while August and September are bearish in bear markets. Furthermore, a breakdown below $56,500 could initially act as a “stop hunt” or bear trap before the true breakdown occurs. The leading scenario involves at least one more low, possibly two, before a final low forms towards the end of the year.

Bitcoin Dominance and the Triangle Pattern

Bitcoin dominance provides crucial context for the broader crypto market. Currently, dominance is trading within a range that can be interpreted as an Elliott Wave triangle. This structure began with a low around 57% in late last year.

The decline starting in June 2025, where dominance topped around 66%, is viewed as the “D wave” of the triangle. The current sideways action suggests we are in the “B wave” of this D wave, which itself may be a smaller triangle. For the dominant bearish bias to continue, the range must break downward.

Key Dominance Levels:

  • Breakout Level: A break below 57.9% (ideally 57%) confirms the next leg down.
  • Target: A breakdown could lead to the low 50% region, potentially triggering an altcoin season.

However, this decline in dominance is unlikely to occur until Bitcoin has firmly established its bear market low. Until then, dominance will likely continue to consolidate.

Altcoin Market Outlook

While a drop in Bitcoin dominance typically signals strength for altcoins, the outlook for the altcoin sector is mixed. It is estimated that 99% of altcoins will not recover their previous all-time highs. Many will die out, and the total number of viable altcoins will shrink over time.

New hype cycles will emerge, and some established coins may perform well, but success will depend on individual chart structures. The potential “altcoin season” linked to the dominance triangle breakdown is realistic but likely won’t materialize until Bitcoin completes its bear market cycle.

Microstructure Scenarios and Short-Term Support

On smaller timeframes, two primary scenarios are being tracked:

  1. Corrective Scenario (Leading): Bitcoin is in a “flat correction” following a five-wave advance in July. The current pullback is the “C wave” of this correction. Support lies between $59,369 and $62,533. A positive reaction here is needed for any subsequent rally.
  2. Bullish Variation: If a substantial low has already formed, the recent rally could be “wave one” of a new impulse, with the current pullback being “wave two.” This scenario would allow for a move toward the $69,000–$72,000 resistance zone.

Currently, short-term support is at $63,752. The market is hovering above this level, and the immediate pressure seems downward. Traders should watch for a reaction at the $59k–$62k support zone to determine the next directional move.
