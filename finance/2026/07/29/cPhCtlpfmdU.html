Pan American Silver is ranked as the lowest-risk option. Recent acquisitions, including a 44% stake in Juanicipio, have strengthened its portfolio, with all-in sustaining costs (AISC) around $6.50 per ounce. The stock has broken out of a decade-long sideways range.

  • Entry Strategy: A conservative entry is at the retest of the demand zone between $33 and $40. A safer trigger is the reclaim of the range high at $44.50.
  • Risk: The worst-case scenario involves a drop to the mid-level support at $26.50.

SilverCrest Metals is the author’s largest personal position. Despite reduced output in China due to safety upgrades, the stock is pulling back to a key support level.

Aya Gold & Silver is a clean, self-funded producer with over $170 million in cash. Its Hounde mine in Morocco recently hit record production, and the Boumadine project is advancing through feasibility studies.
