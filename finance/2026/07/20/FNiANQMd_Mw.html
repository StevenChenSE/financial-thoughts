Live Q&A with Rick Rule and Steve Barton · Rule Investment Media

In this live session, Rick Rule and Steve Barton discuss recent market movements, provide detailed rankings on specific natural resource stocks, and outline future plans for the Rule Classroom and upcoming conferences.

Introduction

Welcome to the Rule Classroom Plus. Steve Barton and Rick Rule join to address questions from the community following the recent Natural Resources Investment Symposium. Rick emphasizes the value of the conference recordings, noting that the breakout sessions offer critical real-world application of investment lessons. He encourages attendees to utilize these resources and hints at future conference improvements, including potential expansions in oil and gas coverage and the “Future Legends” panel.

Equinox Gold: Tactical Mergers and Ross Beaty’s Return

Rick Rule discusses Equinox Gold, acknowledging its recent 50% price drop. He holds the stock as a “4” but is considering an upgrade to a “3” if the price falls further. He critiques Equinox’s recent mergers with Calibre and Orla as tactical moves to increase size rather than strategic ones for operational synergy. However, he remains bullish on the long-term potential.

Rule highlights Ross Beaty, the driving force behind Equinox, who remains active despite claiming retirement. Rule predicts Beaty will sell off third-tier deposits to fund further acquisitions of tier-one assets once the weather turns in British Columbia. He also suggests that the Los Filos mine in Mexico, now politically stable, is a prime candidate for sale to a local operator.

Yukon Explorers: Banyan and Snowline

Addressing questions on Yukon explorers, Rule is long in both Banyan Gold and Snowline Resources. He praises Banyan for its existing infrastructure, including paved roads and proximity to the Victoria Mine. Snowline, while lacking infrastructure, boasts astonishing grades. Rule advises investors to review his pre-conference interviews with both management teams to understand the exploration upside. He notes he will not upgrade Banyan until a Preliminary Economic Assessment (PEA) is released, expected in Q4.

Saturn Metals and the “Orphan” Valuation Anomaly

Rule expresses strong interest in Saturn Metals, holding a reasonable position. He identifies a valuation anomaly: Saturn operates an open-pit heap leach oxide target in Australia, a deposit type well-understood by American analysts but rare in the Australian market. Consequently, American investors avoid it due to location, and Australian investors ignore it due to lack of understanding. Rule values the involvement of Jonathan Goodman and Dundee Precious Metals, whose technical and financial support mitigates risk.

Centaurus Metals and the Nickel Market

Rule rates Centaurus Metals as a “4,” noting it is a speculative play on a nickel deposit that is currently in a “boring” permitting phase. He expects the stock to remain stagnant until final financing and permitting are announced. Regarding the recent pullback in nickel prices, Rule disagrees with the view that inventory drawdowns are the primary cause. Instead, he attributes the price drop to high oil prices acting as an economic tax and higher interest rates increasing the cost of hoarding metal, leading to liquidation of speculative holdings.

Gold, Silver, and Interest Rates

When asked about catalysts for precious metals, Rule points to US interest rates. He draws a parallel to 1975, where high rates initially crushed gold prices but eventually forced the Federal Reserve to cut rates due to economic damage. Rule believes 90% of gold’s current weakness is due to the strong US dollar driven by high nominal interest rates. He expects rates to eventually fall, triggering a significant rise in gold prices.

Other Stock Rankings and Comments

  • Freehold Royalty: Ranked as a “4”. Rule considers it his favorite oil and gas royalty company due to its strong capital allocation and historic royalty base.
  • Mogotes Metals: Rule is cautious but positive about the partnership with Rio Tinto. He notes that Rio Tinto’s geological expertise is invaluable for drilling porphyry deposits, though it may reduce the likelihood of an auction for the asset.
  • Ivanhoe Electric: Rule understands the geological assets but admits he cannot fully evaluate the impact of their I-Pulse technology.
  • Honey Badger: Rule loves the deposit but is wary of the stock’s 350% run-up. He highlights the logistical challenges of an air-only access site and the high cost of operations.
  • Coeur Mining: Rule does not own Coeur due to its history of poor capital allocation and a past lapse in land management at the Rochester mine.
  • Gold Royalty: Ranked as a “4”. Rule sees potential in the balance sheet and expects consolidation in the junior royalty sector.
  • Nexa Resources: Rule likes “silver producers in drag” (zinc/lead producers with high silver byproducts), seeing a latent re-rating potential if the market recognizes their silver content.

Future Content: Pitch Rick and Oil & Gas Basics

Rule announces new content formats for the Rule Classroom. “Pitch Rick” will allow companies to pitch deals in front of the community, while a “Shark Tank” style deep-dive will feature hour-long due diligence sessions with high-quality companies. Additionally, responding to member requests, Rule plans to develop an introductory course on oil and gas basics, potentially in conjunction with the Colorado School of Mines and Keith Hill.

Long-Term Commodity Outlook

Concluding with a question on Doomberg’s view that commodity prices trend lower in real terms over the long run, Rule agrees but with a crucial timeframe distinction. He argues that the “long run” does not include the next 10 years. Due to 30 years of underinvestment in natural resources, Rule predicts a period of high prices and supply constraints. He expects technology and high cash flows to eventually increase supply, validating the long-term bearish view only after a decade of bullish pressure.

Summary

Rick Rule provides a comprehensive update on his stock rankings, highlighting Equinox Gold, Banyan, and Saturn Metals. He emphasizes the critical role of interest rates in driving precious metals prices and predicts a robust commodity market over the next decade due to chronic underinvestment. Viewers are encouraged to utilize the symposium recordings and stay tuned for new educational content on oil and gas.
